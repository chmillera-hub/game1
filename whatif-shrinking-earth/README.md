# What If Earth Shrank… But Kept All Its Mass?

A portrait (1080×1920, 24 fps) animated short. Two sisters, Pip (11) and Tess (20), sit on a
rooftop the night before Tess leaves for college. Pip asks what would happen if everything inside a
bubble around the Earth and the Moon shrank in perfect proportion but kept every gram of mass. Tess
walks her through it and a narrator fills in the physics.

**Deliverables** (in `output/`):

| file | what |
| --- | --- |
| `what_if_earth_shrank.mp4` | main video, H.264 + AAC, under 15 MB, burned-in subtitles |

## The physics in the script

With scale factor *s* (1 = today), mass fixed:

| stage | size | what happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| any *s* | everything ×*s* | an observer who shrinks too sees no change (the Moon keeps its apparent size) |
| *s* = 0.9 | 11,500 km | surface gravity ∝ 1/s² → +23 %; air pressure ∝ 1/s⁴ → ~1.5 atm (ears pop) |
| *s* = 0.5 | Mars-sized | gravity ×4; day length ∝ s² (spin-up, angular momentum) → 6 h; compression heats the planet until it glows |
| *s* = 0.01 | ~130 km | density ~5×10⁹ kg/m³ ≈ white dwarf; a teaspoon ≈ 27 t (four or five elephants); day ≈ 9 s |
| *s* ≈ 0.0035 | ~44 km | equator reaches orbital speed → it flattens and flings off a ring |
| *s* ≈ 2.5×10⁻⁵ | ~300 m | ~4×10¹⁷ kg/m³ ≈ neutron star: electrons are pushed into protons; a teaspoon ≈ 10⁹ t |
| *s* ≈ 1.4×10⁻⁹ | 1.8 cm | Schwarzschild radius 2GM/c² = 8.9 mm → a marble-sized black hole |

At that scale the Moon is ~4.8 mm across (a peppercorn) about 0.5 m away. Its orbit takes
0.12 µs (about 8 million orbits a second) at ~9 % of light speed, and gravitational waves pull it in within milliseconds.
Seen from far away, gravity depends only on mass, so the black hole keeps Earth's orbit, once a year.
The reality check: Earth is far too light to collapse on its own. Even the Sun (333,000 Earth masses)
will end as a white dwarf. Electron degeneracy and electromagnetic repulsion "push back".

## How it is made

Everything is generated by code in `src/`:

- `script.py` — screenplay (lines, pauses, music accents on hopeful/heavy words)
- `tts.py` — voices with Kokoro-82M (Tess `af_heart`, Pip `af_bella` +2.6 st, narrator `bm_fable`),
  word timestamps + phonemes, checked with Whisper for intelligibility
- `timeline.py`, `anchors.py`, `direction.py` — absolute timeline, shot list, music map, SFX cues
- `rig.py`, `poses.py`, `acting.py`, `acting_script.py` — Skia character rig (pseudo-3D head turns,
  lids/brows/iris gaze, phoneme lip-sync, blinks, micro-saccades, breathing, gestures) and the per-word
  performance script
- `scene_roof.py`, `shots_roof.py` — rooftop set and shots; `sky_chalk.py` — "drawn with starlight"
  sky diagrams; `scene_space.py`, `sphere.py` — space scenes (real land-mask Earth); `scene_vignettes.py`,
  `vig_*.py` — illustrated inserts
- `music.py`, `sfx.py`, `audio_mix.py` — procedural score, sound effects, ducking and loudness
- `subtitles.py` — phrase cards with the spoken word highlighted in the speaker's colour, plus SRT
- `render.py`, `encode.py` — parallel frame rendering and the size-capped two-pass encode

Rebuild (Python 3.12 venv with `kokoro`, `skia-python`, `numpy`, `scipy`, `soundfile`, `pyloudnorm`,
`geopandas<1.0`; `espeak-ng` and `ffmpeg` on PATH):

```bash
cd src
python tts.py            # voices  -> build/voice
python timeline.py       # timeline -> build/timeline.json
python audio_mix.py      # score + sfx + mix -> build/audio/mix.wav
python subtitles.py      # SRT -> output/
python encode.py all     # render + encode -> output/
```

Intermediate files go to `$WHATIF_BUILD` (see `paths.py`).
