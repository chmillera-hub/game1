How the trace works

The packet is the information a person’s pain carries: what they need (deeper connection, a village, room to grow) and the conditions that produced the need (isolation, overwork, rent, food, abuse of power from above).

Each route is scored on four tests. Out: does the information leave the person? Intact: does its meaning survive? Sideways: does it reach others in the same situation? Upward: does it reach whoever controls the conditions? The tests multiply, so a single 0 makes the product 0. They are necessary conditions, not sufficient ones: a 1 means change is possible, not guaranteed.

The scores are judgments about where information flows. They are not measurements, and they say nothing about whether a practice helps the person doing it. Several of these routes help people get through hard conditions. The trace only asks whether the information ever reaches anyone who could change those conditions.
