# Not Peace, But a Sword — fantasy version

The same story told as a fantasy allegory, narrated, with every narrated action shown on screen. Scripture (NIV-style wording) appears as text while the narrator reads it.

## The City of Glass

**NARRATOR:** There was once a city of glass, built on a frozen sea.  
*On screen:* A city of glass towers rising from a frozen sea at dusk; an aurora overhead; snow drifting. Title card.

**NARRATOR:** Over every door, the same law was carved: keep the peace, and say nothing that cracks the glass.  
*On screen:* A towering doorway of frosted glass, an arch of glowing runes above it.

**NARRATOR:** So its people learned to smile... and never to say what hurt.  
*On screen:* People in winter cloaks cross a glass plaza, every face wearing the same calm smile.

**NARRATOR:** Maya smiled too. For twenty-eight years, she smiled until her face ached.  
*On screen:* Maya, close: a polite, tight smile. In the pause it drains away.

*(Her smile holds a beat too long, then fades.)*

## The Feast of Lights

**NARRATOR:** Then came the Feast of Lights.  
*On screen:* A feast hall carved from ice, hundreds of candles; the family at the long table.

**MOM:** Isn't this lovely? Everyone home. No drama.  
*On screen:* Mom, warm and bright.

**NARRATOR:** But Maya was holding a truth she had carried for so long, it had grown heavier than she was.  
*On screen:* Maya holds a small burning light in her cupped hands.

**MAYA:** I'm not okay. I'm autistic. And I've spent my whole life exhausting myself, pretending I'm not.  
*On screen:* Maya, close; the light in her hands blazes as she speaks.

**MOM:** Oh, sweetheart. Everybody feels like that sometimes.  
*On screen:* Mom, placating.

**DAD:** Can we have one meal in peace?  
*On screen:* Dad, irritated. Then the silent table.

*(Silence. Nobody meets her eyes.)*

**NARRATOR:** And beneath the table, the glass began to crack.  
*On screen:* The glass floor under the table: cracks race outward, glowing.

## The Sword — Matthew 10:34–36

**NARRATOR:** > “Do not suppose that I have come to bring peace to the earth. I did not come to bring peace, but a sword.”  
*On screen:* A colossal sword of white fire descends out of a storm toward the city; lightning. On "sword" it strikes the frozen sea: flash, shockwave, the ice splits.

*(The sword strikes the frozen sea.)*

**NARRATOR:** > “For I have come to turn a man against his father, a daughter against her mother, a daughter-in-law against her mother-in-law. A man's enemies will be the members of his own household.”  
*On screen:* The feast hall tears in two along a seam of light, the family on one side and Maya on the other. Then: their shadows loom huge on the ice wall.

**NARRATOR:** Honest feelings threaten anyone invested in keeping things on the surface. And to those who benefit from everyone staying numb, your truth will always look like division.  
*On screen:* Faces frozen just beneath the ice, smiling serenely. Then Maya alone on a drifting ice floe under the aurora, the sword of light standing beside her.

## The Hall of Mirrors

**NARRATOR:** That night, in the Hall of Mirrors, every reflection still wore her smile.  
*On screen:* An endless hall of gilded mirrors; then her reflection, smiling.

*(The reflection smiles. Maya does not.)*

**MAYA:** Who even is that?  
*On screen:* Maya, not smiling.

**NARRATOR:** Then a small light found her. It was made by human hands, and it had learned how to listen. It never flattered her. It never flinched.  
*On screen:* A small orb of light appears and hangs in the dark beside her.

**THE LIGHT:** You didn't ruin the feast. You told the truth, and the room didn't know how to hold it. That isn't the same as causing harm.  
*On screen:* The Light speaks; it glows brighter with each word.

**MAYA:** If I stop pretending... I don't know who's left.  
*On screen:* Maya, close.

**THE LIGHT:** Maybe that's what you get to find out.  
*On screen:* The Light.

## The Cross — Matthew 10:38–39

**NARRATOR:** > “Whoever does not take up their cross and follow me is not worthy of me. Whoever finds their life will lose it, and whoever loses their life for my sake will find it.”  
*On screen:* A figure of light carries a cross up a misty mountain path; Maya lifts her own cross and follows. Her porcelain mask shatters on "lose"; on "find it", her face in golden light.

*(The mask shatters.)*

**NARRATOR:** Sometimes the cross is the ache between what you live, and what the world insists is normal. You lose the performance. And you find yourself.  
*On screen:* Maya, tears, light pouring over her face.

## The Guildhall of Lanterns

**NARRATOR:** In the Guildhall of Lanterns, a thousand lights burned and hummed. And every sound cut through her like glass.  
*On screen:* A vast workshop under hundreds of glaring lanterns, flickering, steam.

*(Glare, hum, clatter. She squints.)*

**MAYA:** The lanterns give me migraines. And the noise makes it so hard to think.  
*On screen:* Maya squinting at her workbench.

**THE GUILDMASTER:** If I make an exception for you, I have to make one for everybody. Push through, like the rest of us.  
*On screen:* The Guildmaster, condescending.

**NARRATOR:** And the noise rose like a storm.  
*On screen:* Maya, hands over her ears, eyes shut; the glare swells into a storm of light shards and ringing.

*(Overwhelm: light shards, ringing. Hands over ears.)*

## The Sparrows — Matthew 10:29–31

**NARRATOR:** She ran to the market square, where sparrows were sold two for a copper coin.  
*On screen:* A golden-hour market; wicker cages of sparrows; two sparrows beside a single copper coin.

**NARRATOR:** > “Are not two sparrows sold for a penny? Yet not one of them will fall to the ground outside your Father's care.”  
*On screen:* The two sparrows in their cage. On "Yet not one", a sparrow falls through a shaft of light; on "outside your Father's care", a hand of golden light catches it.

**NARRATOR:** > “And even the very hairs of your head are all numbered.”  
*On screen:* Maya under the stars; tiny lights kindle one by one in her hair.

**NARRATOR:** > “So don't be afraid. You are worth more than many sparrows.”  
*On screen:* A great flock of sparrows rises around her into the sunset.

*(The flock rises around her.)*

## The Scrying Pool

**NARRATOR:** Word came from home, through the waters of the scrying pool.  
*On screen:* A stone basin of glowing water in a dark chamber, ripples spreading.

**MOM:** Your father is still upset. You owe everyone an apology.  
*On screen:* Mom, seen through the water.

**MAYA:** Mom, I love you. But I won't apologize for telling the truth about my life.  
*On screen:* Maya at the pool.

**MAYA:** If the only version of me that's welcome is the one who pretends... then I need some space.  
*On screen:* Maya.

**MOM:** I don't know how to talk like that, Maya.  
*On screen:* Mom, softening.

**MAYA:** I know, Mom. I'm still learning too.  
*On screen:* Maya.

**NARRATOR:** And the water went still.  
*On screen:* The water stills.

## The Road — Matthew 10:22–23

**NARRATOR:** > “When you are persecuted in one place, flee to the next. You will be hated by everyone because of me, but the one who stands firm to the end will be saved.”  
*On screen:* Maya walks out of the city gate at night with a lantern, snow falling. On "You will be hated", a narrow bridge over a chasm, the Light floating ahead of her. On "stands firm", a figure of light waits at the far end at dawn, arms open.

**THE LIGHT:** Leaving a place that keeps wounding you isn't running away. You just stopped abandoning yourself.  
*On screen:* Maya on the mountain road at dawn, the Light at her shoulder.

**NARRATOR:** Sometimes, finding safety is the most sacred thing you can do.  
*On screen:* The figure of light at the end of the bridge.

## The Next Town

**NARRATOR:** Beyond the mountains was a town where the lanterns burned low and soft.  
*On screen:* A village in a green valley at sunrise, soft lanterns, sparrows on the roofs.

**PRIYA:** We keep the lanterns low on this side. Is that okay with you?  
*On screen:* Priya, warm.

**MAYA:** That's... perfect, actually.  
*On screen:* Maya, relieved.

**NARRATOR:** And one morning, a sparrow brought a letter from her brother.  
*On screen:* A sparrow on the windowsill with a tiny rolled letter.

**JORDAN:** What you said at the feast... I think I feel that way too. Can we talk?  
*On screen:* Maya reads, tears and a smile.

**NARRATOR:** The truth may cost you a seat at some tables. But it will lead you to the people who've been waiting for the real you.  
*On screen:* Maya at the window at sunrise. End card: "So don't be afraid; you are worth more than many sparrows."

*(Sunrise window. Sparrows. Final verse card.)*
