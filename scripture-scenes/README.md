# Scripture Scenes

One animated video (about 3 minutes) with four short scenes. The subtitles are the passages exactly as provided.

| Scene | Passage | Spoken by |
|-------|---------|-----------|
| A village quarrel: a neighbor smashes a pot, later comes hungry in the rain and is fed | "Romans 12:20" | Narrator |
| The supper table | John 15:12 | Jesus |
| Paul writing from prison, with a flashback to his past and the road to Damascus | 1 Timothy 1:12-16 | Paul |
| A traveler through storm, famine and danger, to sunrise | Romans 8:35-39 | Narrator |

File: `videos/scripture-scenes.mp4`. Rebuild with `python3 build.py s1` (reuses `../magic-show`).
