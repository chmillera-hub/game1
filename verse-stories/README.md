# Verse Stories

One animated video (about 3.5 minutes). Each line of the four passages gets its own everyday scene with
its own characters. The narrator sets up a situation, and a character (or the narrator) speaks the
verse. Subtitles of the verses are exactly as provided.

- **"Romans 12:20":** a ripped drawing in a school hallway, a shared lunch with a credit-stealing
  coworker (and a chuckling janitor), a tripped soccer player, extra change at a grocery checkout, and
  feuding neighbors with a plate of cookies
- **John 15:12:** a dad reads to siblings fighting over the last dinner roll
- **1 Timothy 1:12-16:** Marcus shares his testimony at a church group, with flashbacks to violence, a
  prison chaplain, and a river baptism; the man he once hurt is there to embrace him
- **Romans 8:35-39:** a hospital room the night before surgery, a hardship montage, a secret
  candlelit prayer meeting, walking out of the hospital, a widower at sunrise, and everyone together at
  a sunset picnic

File: `videos/verse-stories.mp4`. Rebuild with `python3 build.py v1`.
