# Animation rules: anatomy, lighting, visuals (from the client)

Visual fixes only — every word of the script stays exactly as written. Nothing here changes voices, music or sound.
Apply every rule to every character in every shot, background characters included. Every line or shape on a body must match real anatomy or a real clothing fold (delete stray lines and "mud" blobs).

Hands & grips: hands sit in the sleeve holes; hands read as hands (palm, separate fingers, thumb correctly placed), never a blob; no outlines inside the hand that break its structure, no small outlined ovals; held objects sit in the palm with fingers wrapped around, raised to a natural height; a held object hides the part of the hand behind it, thumb never shows through; offered food palm up with thumb/forefinger gripping; palm direction fits the gesture (a hand near the face has the palm toward the face).
Arms, elbows, shoulders: arms attach at the shoulder and are fully formed (upper arm, elbow, forearm, hand), never floating or blob-shaped; no arm clipping through clothing (elbow slightly outward); elbows bend naturally, low and relaxed; no arms dangling in mid-air — resting arms lie on the body/legs, idle arms move slightly.
Necks & heads: realistic neck length, never elongated; the neck angles smoothly toward where the head looks (no Z zigzag); subtle V-shaped sternocleidomastoids from behind each ear to the notch between the collarbones; head centred on neck and shoulders; close-ups include the neck and top of clothing (never a floating head); smooth face contours, no sharp dents.
Faces & eyes: characters in conversation look at the person they address, on the correct side of the screen, not at the ground all the time; speakers mostly hold eye contact; one motivated look per beat; show emotion through the eyes; expressions fit the role (neutral mouths don't read as smiles).
Hair & headwear: blended lifelike hair with flowing strands, no stamped circles or flat mud patches; side locks attach to the scalp and lie against the head, never floating over the ears; headwear sits low and follows the skull, with cloth detail.
Legs, feet, poses: if the lower body is framed, legs are anatomically correct; feet point where the body faces; seated legs agree with the torso; kneeling = knees on ground, garment over knees, feet hidden; clothing over legs shows thighs and knees through folds and shading, never a solid blob.
Clothing: soft gradient shoulder shadows, no hard brown lines; cross-body drapes held up by the shoulder, arm emerging from under; garment colours consistent scene to scene.
Proportions & design: children have shorter neck and torso; characters clearly distinguishable; name labels shown the moment a character first appears.
Motion & continuity: no statue characters — subtle idle motion always; speakers gesture, listeners react; eased head turns; no 1-3 frame jolts or teleports; emotional shaking smooth and low-amplitude; background figures keep moving.
Tears: one object that runs down and fades out as a whole; slightly blue, translucent; start about halfway into the beat; sparing — one tear from one eye for quiet recognition.
Lighting & colour: lighting matches visible sources; a character with a light behind is never near-black (rim + fill); soft gradated shadows, no muddy patches; night/mood tints gentle — never turning clothing a different colour.
Staging, props, environment: every scripted action unmistakable through pose, props and angle (reading = both hands on the letter, eyes on it; a knock shown from outside the door); in two-person exchanges angle characters so it's clear who looks at whom; objects rest on surfaces; doors swing on hinges; brick and stone gritty and weathered.
