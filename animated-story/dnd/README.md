# DO NOT DISTURB

A three-part animated story about Tiredness, Embarrassment, Impulsivity and a creature that should never have left its cage.

| Part | Title | Covers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Thing Under the Door** | Tiredness is gaming, ignores the commotion outside and puts his noise-canceling headphones on. The room shakes, "What the actual f***?", nothing outside. Embarrassment panics outside with an open cage. The thing slips under the door. Embarrassment bangs on the door, circles the house, smashes a window and lands in a heap in front of Impulsivity, a living tripwire. Something scratches up the stairs. Embarrassment bursts in, freezes bright red, rummages in the closet, and turns to find Tiredness right behind him. He leaps into the ceiling, then hides the thing under a sweater. Tiredness asks "How's that new research job going?" and Embarrassment begs him not to tell. He backs out and shuts himself in, Impulsivity bursts in, and there's a cage tug-of-war. Tiredness gets bitten and runs off screaming, leaving "Oh my God. What do I do?" |
| 2 | **The Recorder** | Black vans and a wrecked street. Embarrassment says "Hey boss, it's all good." The boss keeps him only because his friend got bitten, and doesn't tell him that. At Calm Corp (slogan: "Feel less."), Embarrassment sits in a tiny chair while Dr. Contempt dismisses him and forces a hidden recorder on him. He visits Tiredness, tries to stop him saying anything incriminating, trips over Impulsivity, and the recorder falls out ("It's a pager. Pagers are back."). Tiredness plays along, then looks up Calm Corp and decides to walk in the front door. |
| 3 | **The Shaft** | Tiredness gets in through deadpan social engineering ("I'm here about the thing." "What thing?" "Exactly."). He finds the lab file on Subject 7 and leans on the alarm button. He runs from guards, jumps through a window and falls, his life flashing before his eyes ("Nap. Game. Nap."). His powers kick in, then everything goes black. He wakes in the sewers, sensing things no one should. The real creature attacks and he catches it, holds it, and it calms down: they're alike. It follows him, rolls its eyes and pokes him until he takes the right tunnel. It leads him to a shaft full of pods holding creatures like it. "These are your family. Aren't they?" "Okay. I'm awake now." |

**Art style:** "riso noir". Cream paper, indigo ink lines, a few fluorescent spot colors, halftone-dot shading, film grain, and character animation on twos (15 drawings a second) for a hand-made feel.

**Build:** `python3 build.py 1 2 3 --series dnd`. Videos and captions go to `out/do-not-disturb/`.
