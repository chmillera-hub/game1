How it works

Board layout

Top row: slots 1–4. Bottom row: slots 5–7, offset so 5 sits between 1&2, 6 between 2&3, 7 between 3&4.

Corners: 1, 4, 5, 7. Middle: 2, 3, 6. Adjacent = touching (e.g. 3 touches 2, 4, 6, 7).

Opposing row = the other row. Group checks (row, corners, middle) include the card itself unless the card says "another", "other" or "sharing … with this card".

Silver cards

Silver only helps you. For bonuses, a silver card counts as every color at once. Color penalties never trigger on silver. Cards that name silver directly (like "adjacent silver") work as written.

Scoring

Each card scores its face value plus or minus its text. Your score is the sum of all 7. Only submitted boards count toward your best score.

Reading small cards

Tap a card to read it in Card detail. Pinch the board, or use the Zoom buttons, to enlarge it. On a computer, hover a card to enlarge it.
