# Eeyore & the Pasture

A short (~3:46) animated, fully voiced video adapting the essay
*"Winnie-the-Pooh and Friends and Eeyore: a metaphor for how society often mishandles emotional needs."*

**Watch:** [`eeyore-and-the-pasture.mp4`](eeyore-and-the-pasture.mp4) (1920×1080, 30 fps, under 5 minutes)

## Structure (follows the essay's sections)

| # | Section | What happens on screen |
|---|---------|------------------------|
| – | Opening | Title over the Hundred Acre Wood; everyone happy… almost everyone. |
| 1 | The Surface-Level Support | "Come eat with us, Eeyore!" Friends congratulate themselves; Eeyore's inner thoughts in a thought bubble. |
| 2 | The Dismissal | "I want to run in a pasture." Crickets. Subject change, "depressed", "unreasonable", "stay in the forest". His dream bubble pops. |
| 3 | The Pathologization of Joy | A bluebird tells him about the pasture; he smiles for the first time; "Are you… manic?"; "What the hell?" |
| 4 | Misaligned Needs | Forest routines vs. the open pasture; the trees close in; Eeyore torn between them. |
| 5 | The Toxic Cycle | A five-step cycle diagram while Eeyore's rain cloud grows. |
| 6 | The Breaking Point | In the rain: "Pooh… I'm miserable. I want to go run in a field!" Thunder, then silence and sunlight. |
| 7 | The Hard Truth | "But… we love you, Eeyore." Forest shadows fall across him like bars. |
| – | The Takeaway | "This isn't working for me… I need to find what does." He gallops into the pasture. |

## How it's made

Everything is generated by code and can be re-rendered after edits:

- `screenplay.py`: every line, who says it, pauses, and voice casting. **Edit dialogue here.**
- `tts.py`: synthesizes voices with [Piper](https://github.com/rhasspy/piper) (offline neural TTS), pitch-shifts per character, builds the timeline and a per-frame lip-sync envelope.
- `music.py`: synthesizes an original score and sound effects keyed to the timeline, and ducks the music under dialogue.
- `web/`: the animation itself, drawn on an HTML canvas (`chars.js` characters, `bg.js` backgrounds, `scenes.js` choreography). Blinks, eye-darts toward whoever is speaking, eyebrows, eyelids, ears and mouths are all animated.
- `render.js`: renders frames with headless Chromium (Playwright) into MP4.
- `build.sh`: runs the whole pipeline.

Character designs are original drawings in the spirit of the public-domain 1926 A. A. Milne book; they deliberately avoid the Disney designs.
