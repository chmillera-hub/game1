# The Spark in the Storm

A two-part animated short (about 3¼ and 3½ minutes) with voice acting, made
from a written vision: someone sings a beautiful song while the world
abandons them. A listener hears it, and a storm rises in their mind (the
abandonment, the scarcity, the turned backs) until devil horns grow out of it.
Then a tiny divine light appears that the storm can't put out. The listener
takes on a Jesus-like form, kneels before the singer, and walks out into the
night carrying the spark to pass it on.

- `out/part1.mp4`: **Part One, The Song** (3:12)
- `out/part2.mp4`: **Part Two, The Spark** (3:30)

## How it's made

Everything is generated by code. No stock footage, samples or copyrighted
music are used.

| File | What it does |
|---|---|
| `script.py` | The screenplay: shot list, dialogue lines and their timing, thunder hits, song cues |
| `audio.py` | Voices (Piper neural TTS), the original wordless song (formant-synthesis singer, piano, strings, choir, music box), storm/rain/heartbeat effects, and the mix. It also writes the lip-sync envelopes |
| `scene.js` | Characters drawn on a canvas: eyes with saccades, blinks, pupils, tears and drops, brows, lip-sync mouths, horns, halo, the hair, beard and robe transformation |
| `env.js` | The room, the street, the storm, lightning, the demon, the spark, rain, film grain, subtitles |
| `shots.js` | The choreography for each shot of both parts |
| `render.py` | Renders the frames in headless Chromium and encodes them with ffmpeg |

The song "My Dearest" inspired this, but it isn't used. The singer performs an
original melody, sung wordlessly, over the same kind of IV–V–iii–vi
progression that anime ballads often use.

## Rebuild

```bash
pip install piper-tts soundfile scipy numpy playwright
# voices from https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices (en_US-ryan-high,
# en_GB-cori-high, en_US-libritts_r-medium) go in ./voices or $PIPER_VOICES
python3 audio.py          # -> build/partN.wav + build/timeline_partN.json
python3 render.py         # -> out/part1.mp4, out/part2.mp4
python3 render.py --stills 1 60 120   # quick PNG checks
```

For a live preview with sound, serve this folder (`python3 -m http.server`)
and open `scene.html?part=1`.

## Changing things

- Dialogue, timing and voices are in `script.py`. After editing, re-run `audio.py`, then `render.py`.
- Expressions, gestures and camera moves are in `shots.js`. Each shot is a function of time.
- Look and design (faces, clothes, horns, halo) is in `scene.js`. Settings (room, street, storm, spark) are in `env.js`.
