# Production Bible — "The Void and My Fear"

A portrait (9:16, 720x1280) animated short with voice acting, narration, music and SFX,
built entirely in code: Node + @napi-rs/canvas (Skia) for frames, Kokoro neural TTS for voices,
numpy-synthesized music/SFX, ffmpeg for mux/encode.

## The creator's brief (summary of their requirements)

- Portrait mode for phones. As long as needed to tell the whole narrative (expect ~5–7 min).
- Compress hard (target < 15 MB total); slight quality loss OK if coherent and readable.
- Voice acting + narration + background music to deepen emotional resonance.
- The story text below is a GUIDE: use the words directly when they work, be creative where it helps.
- PRIORITY: facial movements (eyes, eyelids, brows, mouth), body language, emotionally in-depth
  dialogue, and characters reacting to dialogue with nuance and precision. They prefer conversation
  and narration over wild, outlandish animation.
- Dialogue on the SLOWER side — leave processing time and room for faces/bodies to carry emotion.
- First frame IS the video (no fade in from black) — it is the social-media preview still, so the
  first frame must be a beautiful, legible, inviting image.
- Experimental/surprising choices are welcome; the creator will request changes afterward.

### Required story beats (all must appear, in this order)
1. The narrator remembers the first story they wrote about their emotions; inside their mind (Inside-Out-like HQ).
2. Fear keeps pulling the fire alarm.
3. The narrator imagines themself as the CEO of their mind, looking around to see why.
4. The Void: huge, intimidating, standing in the back of the mind, unmoving, face pure darkness.
5. Fear believes the presence will harm it — it's not safe.
6. Fear keeps pulling the alarm, panic attacks, forcing all emotions out of the mind — chaos.
7. One day Consciousness puts a hand on shaking Fear's shoulder: "You need to stop pulling the fire alarm. It's causing turmoil."
8. Fear refuses and pulls the alarm again.
9. "Fear, we need to put you in a time-out." Fear goes in a cage, crying, shaking, pulling at bars. The narrator feels sadness.
10. The Void stands stone-faced; its dark shadow takes up a large part of the room.
11. While the narrator isn't looking — an image: the Void's hand unlocks the cage.
12. Fear freezes, looks at the Void: "Why are you doing this? You're going to hurt me. This is a trick. This can't be right."
13. The Void does not move; stares off into the distance.
14. Fear cries: "This doesn't make any sense. You're supposed to hurt me. Why are you just standing there? Are you just here to scare me forever?" The Void does not move.
15. The Void turns its gaze to Fear. Fear thinks this is the end; hides its head in its hands.
16. A hand on Fear's shoulder. Fear looks up into the Void's face: not darkness — twinkling lights, galaxies, nebulae, stars; the void filled with bits and pieces of meaning. Tears in Fear's eyes.
17. Wonder and amazement replace the expectation of harm.
18. Fear: "Why are you showing me this?" The Void lifts its hand away, turns, stares into the distance — toward the screen of consciousness.
19. Fear looks where the Void looks: the other emotions are looking back. They smile briefly, then go back to work helping Consciousness.
20. Fear wipes its tears; feels the Void's gentle push toward the emotions.
21. Fear looks over its shoulder at the Void — galaxies twinkling in its dark facade. Fear smiles and returns to work with the emotions.
22. The cage: gate rusted, hanging open — a small detail.
23. Scene change: emotions pressing console buttons, Void standing in the background, the rusty time-out cage barely visible in the back of the mind.
24. "After that, I may have shed a tear or two myself."

## Cast (original designs — evocative of, but NOT copies of, any film's characters)

- **NARRATOR** (voice-over) — the storyteller, the "I", speaking gently in past tense, reflective,
  unhurried, warm. Same voice as CONSCIOUSNESS. Gender-neutral presentation.
- **SELF** — the narrator in the real world: a young adult at a desk at night with an old notebook
  ("my first story"). Soft rounded human design, short tousled dark hair, cozy oversized sweater
  (dusty teal), expressive almond eyes, visible eyelids and brows. Same face as CONSCIOUSNESS.
- **CONSCIOUSNESS** ("the CEO") — the narrator's self inside the mind. Same face as SELF but wearing
  a slightly-too-big charcoal blazer over the sweater, a small gold "CEO" lapel badge, and a clipboard
  at first. Kind, tired, trying to be responsible. Speaks with Fear.
- **FEAR** — small (about 55% of Consciousness's height), pale lavender, soft teardrop/bean body,
  oversized round eyes with big irises and heavy expressive upper lids, thin worried brows, a wobbly
  tuft of hair/antenna, thin arms and knobbly legs, a little knitted scarf. Trembles when scared.
  Voice: small, breathy, quick to crack; it is the emotional center of the film.
- **JOY** (warm gold, star-shaped tuft), **SADNESS** (soft blue, droopy round), **ANGER** (coral red,
  square & stocky), **CALM** (mint green, tall & willowy) — the "other emotions". They work the
  CONSOLE. Mostly reactive; they may have a few very short lines (e.g. during the evacuation).
- **THE VOID** — enormous (fills 60–80% of frame height in wide shots), a tall, hooded, cloaked
  presence of deep blue-black with a faint violet rim light. Its face is a hood opening of PURE
  DARKNESS (no eyes). Huge, slow, gentle hands (only revealed when they move). NEVER SPEAKS. It
  communicates only through stillness, head turns, a hand on a shoulder, and finally the cosmos
  revealed inside its face: twinkling stars, swirling galaxies, nebulae, and tiny drifting glyphs of
  meaning (a little sun, a house, a heart, a music note, a pencil, a handprint...).

## Locations

- **REAL_ROOM** — night bedroom corner: desk, warm lamp pool, window with soft blue night, the old
  notebook with a childlike cover. Cozy, quiet.
- **MIND_HQ** — the control room inside the mind. Front: the CONSOLE (rows of soft glowing buttons
  and levers) facing the SCREEN OF CONSCIOUSNESS (large window/screen showing a soft view of the
  outside world). On a side wall: the red FIRE ALARM pull station with a strobe/bell above it. EXIT
  door(s) where emotions get herded out. Behind everything: the BACK OF THE MIND — a deep, dim,
  cavernous area where THE VOID stands, its shadow spilling across the floor. The TIME-OUT CAGE (a
  small birdcage-like enclosure with a little hanging "TIME-OUT" sign) sits in the back area near the Void.
- **VOID_FACE** — close-ups inside/at the Void's hood: darkness → cosmos.

Portrait framing makes vertical compositions natural: the Void towering above, Fear tiny below.

## Technical / pipeline constraints (for writers and animators)

- 2D vector animation, 24 fps, 720x1280. Camera can: cut, slow push-in/pull-out, pan, gentle drift,
  crossfade/dissolve. Keep motion gentle — subtle, readable motion compresses well; big full-screen
  flashing/noise does not (keep strobe red light flashes soft/partial).
- Keep ≤ 6 characters on screen. No crowds. No lip-sync for the Void (it never speaks).
- Facial acting vocabulary available per character: gaze direction (eyes move independently of
  head), blinks (full/half/slow/double), upper & lower eyelid positions (wide, sleepy, squint,
  sad-droop), brows (raise, furrow, inner-up "worried", asymmetric), mouth (lip-sync visemes plus
  smile/frown/trembling/pressed lips/open gasp), cheeks blush, tears (welling, single drop, streams,
  wiping), head tilt/nod/shake, breathing, shoulder rise/fall, trembling, hand gestures, hugs-self,
  hiding face in hands, peeking through fingers.
- Speech is slowed (TTS speed ~0.85) with explicit pauses between lines. Use punctuation for rhythm.
- First frame must be a finished, inviting image (no fade from black). A title may sit on the first
  shot and fade OUT after a few seconds.
