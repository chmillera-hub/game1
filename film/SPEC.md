# THE STONE AND THE BREAD — Production Spec

This is the contract every agent works from. The script itself is in `SCRIPT.md` (verbatim from the
user — the dialogue is never changed). This file adds the technical pipeline, the art direction (a
**mythic medieval-fantasy cartoon world**), and a **beat sheet** with target timings. When the two
disagree, the script wins on *story content and dialogue*; this spec wins on *how it looks and is built*.

## 0. The creative direction (from the user)

> "Make the style like a fancy cartoon — avoid basic human figures. Like Zootopia makes them all animals
> and Inside Out makes them all emotion characters. Fantasy theme — not World of Warcraft, but one
> character is a dragon, one is a lion, one is an orc — characters from a fantasy realm in medieval
> times, with a mythic quality."

So: **no humans at all.** Every role is played by an anthropomorphic mythic creature, in a whimsical,
storybook **medieval-fantasy town** (cobblestones, half-timbered houses, lanterns, a castle on the hill).
The script's modern institutions are translated into magical medieval equivalents that keep every gag
and every line of dialogue working (Zootopia logic: the world has its own versions of our things).
Feature-animation polish ("fancy cartoon"): rich light, depth, texture, appealing characters with
secondary motion — think a premium 2D animated short / illustrated storybook, warm and witty, never
grimdark, never MMO-gritty.

### 0.1 PERIOD RULE — it is "back in the day", NOT a modern city (user requirement, overrides everything)

> "Ensure the script isn't taking place in a modern city but back in the day, like Medieval Times —
> like the movie Shrek or Robin Hood, that kind of era."

The world is a storybook **medieval kingdom** — think Disney's *Robin Hood* (animals in medieval
Nottingham) and *Shrek*'s fairy-tale kingdom: thatched and half-timbered cottages, cobbles and mud
lanes, a castle with banners on the hill, market stalls, wells, wooden carts, candles, torches, oil
lanterns, hearth fires, leaded diamond-pane windows, wood, iron, stone, wool, linen, leather, parchment.
**Banned anywhere on screen:** electric lights or bulbs, neon, power lines, plastic, cars, asphalt,
modern glass storefronts, modern signage/typography (no sans-serif shop signs — signs are carved or
painted wood with storybook serif/uncial lettering), modern clothes (hoodies, tees, sneakers, baseball
caps, jeans), modern devices (phones, earbuds, headphones, cameras, screens), modern packaging,
reflective safety gear, zippers.
The script's modern ideas survive only as **in-world magical/medieval equivalents** (that's the comedy,
Shrek-style): the "phone" is an enchanted speaking stone, the "earbuds" are humming enchanted
sea-shells, the "phones raised to film" are scrying crystals, the "dryers" are enchanted tumbling
drums, the "string lights" are strings of candle-lanterns, the "register" is a brass till with a bell.
Any glow from magic objects is soft and arcane (runes, wisps), never a modern screen/UI look: on-screen
text (the speaking stone's menu, the "on hold" timer) is glowing calligraphic rune-lettering or
illuminated script, and the timer is an hourglass. Clothing is medieval: tunics, cloaks, hoods, aprons,
kirtles, jerkins, shawls, boots. Captions (outside the world) may stay in Inter.

**Superseding notes (if any earlier instruction says otherwise, THIS wins):**
- The dwarf's "work vest" is a **sturdy orange-brown tanned-leather work vest** with brass buckles and
  tool loops — NOT a high-visibility/reflective vest.
- The teen faun wears a **red wool hood/cowl and a patched tunic**, a little wooden reed-pipe on a cord
  around his neck and a satchel — NO headphones.
- The elf wears a **hooded travelling cloak/kirtle** (not a hoodie); her "earbuds" are tiny glowing
  enchanted sea-shells in her ears.
- The griffin courier wears a **courier's felt cap with a feather** and a leather parcel satchel with
  a wax seal (royal post), not a modern cap.
- The stone troll wears a **leather jerkin**, not a modern vest or tee.
- Lantern posts are iron posts with **candle/oil flames** in a glass-and-iron lantern; the tavern's
  "string lights" are strings of small paper/candle lanterns.

**Thematic gift of the translation:** the city keeps handing him *stones* — his "phone" is a speaking
rune-stone, the bouncer is a stone troll, the remedies are crystals and cold glass — until a tiny
gnome grandmother hands him *bread*.

### Scene module contract

Each `film/scenes/sN_*.py` defines:

```python
SCENE = 3                      # scene number (1..9)
def setup():                   # optional, called once per process; return precomputed state (cached layers etc.)
    ...
def draw(cr, t, state):        # draw ONE frame. cr = cairo.Context on a 1920x1080 ARGB32 surface,
    ...                        # t = LOCAL scene time in seconds (0 <= t < duration). Must fully paint
                               # the frame (no reliance on previous frames). Deterministic: seed any
                               # randomness (random.Random(1234)), never wall-clock time.
```

Scene duration is `timeline.scene(SCENE).duration`; line timing from `timeline.line(id)` (`.start`,
`.end`, local); visual beats from `timeline.beat(id)` (`.t`, `.end`, local). Extra beats are constants in
the scene file defined relative to existing lines/beats.

### render.py

- `python3 film/render.py --scene 3` → `film/build/video/s3.mp4` (video only, exactly
  `round(duration*24)` frames), raw BGRA (`cairo.FORMAT_ARGB32` little-endian = `bgra`) piped to ffmpeg
  (`-f rawvideo -pix_fmt bgra -s 1920x1080 -r 24 -i -`, out `-c:v libx264 -crf 16 -pix_fmt yuv420p`).
- `--preview [--times 0,2.5,10]` → PNG keyframes `film/build/preview/s3_t002.50.png` (default: every 2 s,
  every line start + 0.3 s, every beat). `--contact` → one contact sheet `film/build/preview/s3_contact.png`.
- `--all [--jobs 4]` renders every scene in parallel processes. `--sheet` renders the character sheets.

### Captions

Burned in by `assemble.py` from `timeline.json` (ASS): Inter SemiBold ~46 px, white, soft dark
outline/shadow, bottom-center, 80 px margin; shown start → end + 0.25 s. `"caption": false` lines are not
captioned (the PHONE lines: scene 3 shows them as floating glowing rune-text instead; the laugh).
Voiceover lines (`"vo": true`) are italic. The drowned line in scene 4 fades out as the music swells.
Keep the bottom ~180 px free of critical detail. No speaker labels.

## 4. Art direction — "fancy cartoon", mythic medieval fantasy

**Look:** premium 2D feature-animation / storybook illustration. Shape language with clear silhouettes;
soft cel-shading (base + one shadow tone + a rim/bounce light) on characters; painterly backgrounds with
gradients, texture (stone blocks, wood grain, cobbles, plaster), atmospheric perspective (far layers
lighter/bluer and slightly blurred = depth of field), contact shadows and ambient occlusion where things
meet the ground. Light is the storyteller: glows and **bloom** on light sources, god-ray light shafts
through windows, drifting particles (dust motes, rain, embers, fireflies, crumbs). Subtle film grain
(3–5 %) and a soft vignette on every frame. Lines: no black outlines on backgrounds; characters may
use a thin outline a few shades darker than their fill (decide once in STYLE.md, apply everywhere).

**Animation principles:** ease-in/out on everything, anticipation and follow-through, squash & stretch
(subtle), overlapping secondary motion (tails, wings, ears, cloaks, scarves, hair, the gnome's hood tip),
idle breathing, blinking, weight shifts. Nobody is ever a frozen cut-out.

**Camera:** gentle slow moves (push-ins, pans, pull-backs) with parallax between layers. Hard cuts
between scenes; scene 1 fades in from black (~1 s); scene 8 fades to black; end card fades in/out. Inside
a scene, an insert close-up (note, stamp, sign) may be a hard cut or a quick push.

## 5. Audio direction

- Dialogue: Piper neural TTS, one consistent voice per character (cast in `audio/tts.py`, documented in
  timeline.json `"cast"`). MAN (dragon): warm, low-key, slightly weary male. OLD_WOMAN (gnome): warm,
  older-sounding female, slower pace, dry wit. PHARMACIST (lioness): kind female, soft. PHONE (speaking
  stone): crisp corporate female, band-limited + a faint magical shimmer. BOUNCER (troll): very deep,
  slow, flat male. CROWD (pixie): young, quick, sneering. Scene 8 voiceover: same MAN voice, slower,
  intimate, a touch of room reverb.
- Ambience/foley, procedural (numpy) or TTS-derived (crowd walla/laughs/cheers from many layered
  LibriTTS-R/VCTK speakers at low level, pitch/time-jittered): S1 wooden drums rumbling + tumbling
  thumps + buzzing wisp-lamp hum; S2 humming light-orbs, glass clinks, items set down on wood, a
  register-like "ka-ching" (brass till bell); S3 rain on cobbles, gutters, distant thunder, the
  **hold music: a tinny music-box playing the same four notes over and over** (through a "stone phone"
  band-pass); S4 lute/fiddle/drum tavern jig through a door → loud inside, synchronized laughter at one
  level, stamp thunk, music SWELLS to drown his line, door closes → muffled; S5 square ambience, cheering,
  sudden silence at the hug; S6 dusk garden: birds, pigeon coos, wing flaps, distant bells, his laugh +
  a soft crackle of embers; S7 pigeons flutter, bread crust tearing, bench scrape on gravel, a warm
  murmur that grows; S8 night crickets, soft conversation murmur, voiceover; S9 silence + final chord.
- **Score:** a gentle piano/harp/celesta (additive synthesis: decaying partials, slight detune, soft
  hammer noise, sustain tail) entering quietly at the laugh in S6 and blooming in S7–S8. Its theme is
  **the same four notes as the hold music**, but slow, warm, harmonized — the stone turned into bread.
  Ends on a held chord under the end card.
- Mix: dialogue always intelligible (duck ambience/music −8 to −12 dB under lines, except the
  intentionally drowned S4 line). Peak ≤ −1 dBFS, integrated loudness ≈ −16 LUFS.

## 7. Quality bar

- Every beat in the script happens on screen, recognisably, in order. Sound off: the story still reads.
  Captions off: the story still reads by sound.
- It looks like a *fancy cartoon*: appealing, polished, lit, textured, with depth — not clip-art, not
  flat placeholder shapes, and **no human characters anywhere**.
- Characters are on-model across scenes (same colors, proportions, costumes).
- No glitches: no flicker, no popping layers, no z-order errors, no text overflowing or cut off, nothing
  important under the caption band, no frozen frames, mouths move only when that character speaks.
- Deterministic and reproducible from `python3 film/make.py`.
