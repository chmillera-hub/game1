# THE STONE AND THE BREAD

**SCENE 1 — The laundromat (0:00–0:25)**
*Visual: open directly on a late-night laundromat, already in motion. Rows of dryers spinning. A man sits alone on a plastic chair watching his clothes tumble. One other person is there, earbuds in, staring at her phone. He leans over to say something, and she taps a sign on the wall, eyes still on her screen: SELF-SERVICE.*

**MAN:** Self-service.
*(he looks around at the spinning machines)*
**MAN:** That's the whole city, isn't it.

**SCENE 2 — The pharmacy (0:25–0:50)**
*Visual: bright fluorescent pharmacy counter. He slides a note across: "Something for loneliness?" The pharmacist reads it, nods kindly, and walks the aisles: sleep aids, vitamins, a stress-relief candle, a coloring book for adults. She stacks them on the counter. He stares at the pile.*

**PHARMACIST:** Try these. Lots of people swear by them.
**MAN:** Do any of them talk back?
*(she pauses, then rings him up anyway)*

**SCENE 3 — The phone menu (0:50–1:10)**
*Visual: he's walking down a rainy street, phone to his ear. Floating text shows the automated voice.*

**PHONE VOICE:** For billing, press one. For a new account, press two. For loneliness… please hold.
*(hold music plays — the same four notes, over and over)*
**MAN:** I've been on hold my whole life.

**SCENE 4 — The bar door (1:10–1:35)**
*Visual: a loud bar glowing with string lights. A bouncer at the door holds up a stamp. He stamps the man's hand, and the stamp reads SMILE. Inside, everyone is laughing at the same volume. The man opens his mouth to say something real, and the music swells and drowns him out. A few heads turn. Someone looks at the bouncer.*

**BOUNCER:** Keep it light, buddy.
*(the man looks at the stamp on his hand, then quietly walks back out)*

**SCENE 5 — The crowd (1:35–2:00)**
*Visual: a street corner. A circle of people with phones raised, filming two guys shoving each other. Cheering. Then one of the guys suddenly stops, drops his arms, and hugs the other. The crowd goes silent. Phones lower.*

**SOMEONE IN THE CROWD:** Ew. What is he doing?
**MAN** *(watching from across the street):* We'll film a fight.
**MAN:** But someone gets gentle, and everybody looks away.

**SCENE 6 — The bench (2:00–2:30)**
*Visual: a nearly empty park at dusk. An old woman sits on a bench tossing bread crumbs to pigeons. He sits at the other end. Long pause. The pigeons look back and forth between them. He takes out the coloring book from the pharmacy, flips through it, sets it down. She glances over.*

**OLD WOMAN:** They tell you to try that too?
**MAN:** Candles. Vitamins. Yoga.
**OLD WOMAN:** They told me to get a cat. I got the cat.
*(beat)*
**OLD WOMAN:** The cat sleeps through every story about my day.
*(he laughs, the first time in the whole video)*

**SCENE 7 — The bread (2:30–3:00)**
*Visual: she tears her loaf in half and hands him a piece. They feed the pigeons together. A teenager walking past slows down and watches. The old woman tears off another piece and holds it out. The teenager hesitates, then sits. Then a tired delivery driver on a break. Then a man in a work vest. The bench fills up. Someone drags over a second bench.*

**MAN:** You know what's funny?
**MAN:** All that stuff they gave me was for being alone.
**OLD WOMAN** *(handing out the last of the bread):* And all this costs is a loaf of bread.

**SCENE 8 — Close (3:00–3:20)**
*Visual: wide shot. The park is dark now except for a single streetlight over the crowded benches. People are talking, leaning in. The laundromat sign, the pharmacy, the bar all glow in the distance, small and far away.*

**MAN** *(voiceover, quiet):* They kept handing me stones.
**MAN:** We were hungry for bread the whole time.
**MAN:** All it took was somebody to break it.

**End card**
> THE STONE AND THE BREAD
> *"Which of you, if your son asks for bread, will give him a stone?" — Matthew 7:9*
