How it works

Each shape's points sit at value × direction from the origin, one point per dimension. The convex "skin" wrapping those points becomes the crystal facets. If the points are too flat to wrap (e.g. many zero values), a smooth ribbon is drawn through them instead. Click any crystal to see its hidden vector data.
