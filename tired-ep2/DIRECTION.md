# DIRECTION — "TIREDNESS — Episode 2: Lights Out"

Read SPEC.md first, especially §0 LESSONS. Real timings are in `out/timeline.json`.
Each scene may grow by its allowance through `engine/scriptedit.update_scene`
(pause beats only). The film must stay ≤ 4:45.

## Pacing and acting (the whole film)
* **Breathe.** Every line gets a beat before it (the listener's face
  anticipating) and after it (the reaction landing). Most shots last 2.5–5 s.
  Cut on story, not on a timer. A held two-shot where only eyes move is
  great.
* **Subtle beats hit hardest.**
  * Pupils slide first; the head follows about 0.15 s later.
  * Slow blinks (0.4 s close, hold, 0.4 s open).
  * One brow lifting a hair.
  * Lip presses.
  * Shoulders rising on a held breath.
  * A creature's ears lowering degree by degree.
* **Big moves are rare:** the bonk on a pipe, the case smash, the lever haul,
  the flop on the bed. Give each a clear anticipation → action → settle.
* **Faces are never frozen.** Rigs blink and drift on their own; add tiny
  moves in holds.
* **Tiredness's lids** rest at about 45% closed.
  * With the hood over his eyes in s02–s03 we read his brows and mouth.
  * His lids lift a little at small surprises.
  * They open FULLY only on "I'm not tired" (s06). Nowhere else.
* **Curiosity carries the comedy and the heart.** Its ears, eyes and tail
  are its voice:
  * ears up for hope, ears flat for hurt, a slow sassy eye-roll, a mischievous
    troll grin;
  * it tilts its head when curious;
  * its tail tip curls when happy.
  It never speaks; its sounds are chirps, chitters and purrs.
* Staging for phones:
  * one subject per shot;
  * medium shots s≈1.3–1.8 and close-ups s≈2.4–3;
  * faces in y 160..1300;
  * the caption band (y ≈1330–1560) kept calm.
* Darkness reads as almost-black with readable shapes. Never a fully black
  frame except where noted.

## s01 — the empty pod (≈33 s, allowance +3 s) — "bond"
* **Frame 0 (bright):** a close-up of Curiosity's face, lit teal from the pod in
  front of it, one paw pressed to the glass of an EMPTY pod. Its eyes are big
  and soft. This is the thumbnail, so it must be bright and beautiful.
* **open:** hold. Its breath fogs the glass in a small circle that fades.
* **pod:** a wider shot on the catwalk. Tiredness (sewer outfit, bandage, teal
  irises at power≈0.6) walks up and stops beside it. Pupils go pod → creature
  → pod.
* **s01_l01** "So this is where they kept you." Soft and quiet.
* **touch:** Curiosity gives a slow nod, ears lowering a little.
* **plate:** an insert of the pod's nameplate, dusty. Tiredness's thumb wipes
  it, revealing: SPECIMEN 00 — CURIOSITY.
* **s01_l02** "...Curiosity.": he reads it slowly.
* **perk:** Curiosity's ears shoot UP and its eyes widen and brighten. It knows
  that name. Name tag **CURIOSITY** pops in (fx.name_tag).
* **s01_l03** "Huh. That fits.": deadpan, then the faintest smile. It just
  dragged him across a sewer.
* **grin:** Curiosity grins proudly, tail curling.
* **plates:** a slow pan along the neighbouring pods. Each sleeper has a
  readable nameplate: JOY, COURAGE, CALM, WONDER, HOPE. Keep each plate in
  frame long enough to read.
* **frown:** back on Tiredness. The meaning lands: inner brows up a hair, then
  a lip press. Silence.
* **camera:** a red light blinks on high on the wall. A security camera's lens
  whirs and swivels to look at them (camera_whir SFX). Tiredness's pupils
  slide up to it.
* Cut to the **control room:** dark, the Boss lit by a wall of monitors, one of
  which shows the two of them.
  * **s01_l04** "Right on time.": no surprise at all. Mystique.
* **reach:** her finger rests on a switch labelled SHAFT POWER.
* **s01_l05** "Lights out." **click.**
* **dark:** back in the shaft, the lights die section by section in a cascade
  of heavy "chunk" sounds, from the far top down toward us. The pod glows dim
  to faint embers. In the end only two pairs of teal eyes are left in the
  dark (Curiosity's large and bright, Tiredness's faint), and they blink.

## s02 — close your eyes (≈34 s, +3 s) — "ominous" warming
* **eyes:** near-black, just the two pairs of eyes, held.
* **s02_l01** "Great. Now I can't see anything.": flat.
* **nudge:** Curiosity's glow moves as it climbs up beside him (we see its teal
  rim), stands on its hind legs and reaches for his head.
* **s02_l02** "Hey. What are you doing?"
* **hood:** it tugs his hood UP and over his eyes (human `hood` 0→1). His eyes
  are now covered; we read his brows and mouth. A beat of annoyance, then a
  sigh. He's too tired to fight it.
* **ping:** silence. Then a deep soft *whoom* (sonar_ping). A teal ripple
  ring expands from his head and, inside the ring band, reveals the world in
  teal: catwalk, rails, stairs, pods (fx sense reveal). It fades behind the
  ring. A second ping follows.
* **s02_l03** "Oh.": surprised, brows lifting under the hood.
* **look:** he turns his head slowly left and right, "looking" with his eyes
  covered, and the pings follow his head.
* **s02_l04** "So I just... close my eyes?"
* **nod:** Curiosity nods (revealed by a ping).
* **s02_l05** "Finally. A superpower I'm actually good at.": deadpan pride, a
  tiny smug smile. The music turns warm around here.
* **smug:** Curiosity gives a slow sassy eye-roll at his smugness.
* **walk:** he walks confidently along the catwalk and down a short stair in
  the dark, pings guiding each step. A nice moment: he's competent.
* **bonk:** he walks face-first into a low pipe the pings didn't reach in time:
  a clear BONK, his head knocked back, a held beat.
* **s02_l06** "...Mostly good at.": flat. Curiosity covers its smile with a paw.

## s03 — flashlights (≈23 s, +4 s) — "sneaky"
* **beams:** the lower level, dark, among pipes and crates. Two flashlight
  beams sweep; the cones reveal the set normally (lit inside the cone). Two
  guards come down the stair.
* **s03_l01** (gruff guard): "The boss says they're down here somewhere."
* **hide:** our two duck behind a big crate. Hood up, he senses (small, quiet
  pings). Curiosity is pressed flat beside him.
* **s03_l02** (scared guard): "I hate the dark." He jumps at his own shadow.
* **sneak:** they sneak along behind the guards in an upright crouch (NOT
  butt-up), in short careful moves, freezing when a beam passes near.
* **troll:** Curiosity stops right behind the gruff guard's head and gives him a
  slow evil troll grin and a little paw wave. Tiredness, sensing it, freezes
  in horror (mouth a flat line, brows up).
* **s03_l03** (whisper) "Stop that."
* **turn:** the guard starts to turn, flashlight swinging round.
* **melt:** at the last instant Curiosity melts into a flat shadow puddle on
  the floor (pose "puddle"). The beam passes over: just floor, just pipes.
* **s03_l04** "Huh. Just pipes." He shrugs and walks off with the other guard.
* **reform:** the puddle rises back into Curiosity, smug, and it does a tiny
  happy wiggle. Tiredness slowly exhales, lips pressed, and gives it a
  deadpan look.

## s04 — not my problem (≈33 s, +3 s) — "lonely"
* **corridor:** a quiet service tunnel with dim emergency lamps. Some light at
  last, so the hood is down. Tiredness stops and sits on a crate, elbows on
  his knees.
* **s04_l01** "Okay. I'm done."
* **slump:** his head hangs, then he rubs his face.
* **s04_l02:** tired, honest, not angry.
* **tug:** Curiosity gently tugs his sleeve and points back down the tunnel
  toward the pods with pleading eyes. It looks up at him.
* **s04_l03** "Look. I'm sorry about your family.": gentle. **beat.**
* **s04_l04** "But they're not my problem.": quiet. He doesn't look at it.
* **hurt:** hold on Curiosity. Its ears lower slowly, degree by degree. Its
  eyes glisten. It lets go of his sleeve. This is the hardest-hitting subtle
  moment in the film, so give it time.
* **leave:** it turns and walks away alone into the dark, its glow shrinking.
  Near the end it stops, looks back once, then goes.
* **alone:** Tiredness alone, with a drip echoing. He lifts his bandaged forearm:
  the bandage glows faintly teal for a moment (their connection). A lip
  press. He looks down the dark tunnel.
* **s04_l05** "...Ugh.": a long reluctant groan.
* **follow:** he stands up and walks after it.

## s05 — the pocket calculator (≈37 s, +3 s) — "awkward"
* **catch:** Tiredness catches up with Curiosity at a junction. It looks up at
  him, surprised and hopeful (ears half up). He looks away.
* **steps:** footsteps approach. A flashlight beam swings in, and it's Embarrassment
  (lab coat, flashlight, recorder LED blinking red in his chest pocket).
* **s05_l01:** startled, blush 0.5. Calmer than Episode 1: it's a whisper-shout.
* **s05_l02** "Hi. Long story.": flat.
* **notice:** Emb's flashlight drifts down to Curiosity. He freezes.
* **s05_l03, s05_l04:** his eyes go creature → Tiredness's glowing eyes.
* **s05_l05** "I fell out of a window.": deadpan.
* **blink:** Emb gives a long slow blink while he processes.
* **led:** the red LED blinks in his pocket. His eyes slide down to it. Then
  Tiredness's eyes go there, then Curiosity's. All three look at the pocket.
* **conflict:** Emb takes the recorder out and holds it in his palm. He looks
  at it, at Tiredness, at it. His blush rises and falls. Then he presses the
  button: click, the LED goes dark.
* **s05_l06:** a deadpan callback, delivered slowly, and for once he is the
  calm one.
* **smile:** Tiredness's faintest smile. Curiosity grins.
* **s05_l07:** he hands over his keycard. **card:** an insert of the card ("LVL 9").
* **s05_l08** "And Tiredness? Be careful.": sincere and slow. **nod:**
  Tiredness gives a small nod.

## s06 — the offer (≈36 s, +3 s) — "lullaby"
* **doors:** a keycard beep, and the heavy doors slide open on the control room: a
  wall of monitors, and the MASTER RELEASE lever in a glass case.
* **chair:** the tall chair facing the monitors slowly turns round. The Boss is
  already there.
* **s06_l01:** calm. **s06_l02:** Tiredness, flat but wary.
* **s06_l03** "I knew the moment it bit you.": mystique. HOW?
* **bandage:** Tiredness's eyes go to his bandage, then back to her.
* **s06_l04–l06:** the offer, delivered softly, almost kindly. The lullaby cue
  makes it feel like a temptation.
* **tempt:** Tiredness is tempted.
  * His lids droop; a small daydream bubble shows his bed with soft light and
    a fluffy pillow.
  * He sways a little. It's what he wanted all along.
* **look:** he looks down at Curiosity. It looks up at him, ears down, hoping.
* **s06_l07** "...That does sound nice.": Curiosity's ears droop further (**droop**).
* **s06_l08** "But for once... I'm not tired.": on "not tired" his lids open
  FULLY for the first time in either episode. Teal irises. A slow push-in.
* **eyes_open:** hold on his open eyes. Then her lids narrow 2 px.

## s07 — release (≈24 s, +4 s) — "chaos"
* **smash:** he punches the glass case (anticipation, then the punch; the glass
  bursts in ≤15 shards).
* **pull:**
  * He grabs the lever with both hands and hauls DOWN.
  * His feet are braced and he strains: teeth gritted, teal glow, the metal
    groaning.
  * The lever moves.
* **button:** the Boss calmly presses LOCKDOWN. **s07_l01** "Lockdown."
* **shutters:** on the monitors and through the window, all the pods begin to
  open... then heavy shutters slam down tier by tier and every pod re-seals.
  Sparks.
* **one_pod:** except one tiny pod at the bottom. Its seal hisses open (pod
  label: JOY).
* **baby:** a tiny baby creature tumbles out, blinks, wobbles and giggles.
  Curiosity rushes to it and nuzzles it.
* **alarm:** red alarm wash, guard footsteps approaching.
* **s07_l02:** Embarrassment at a side hatch, waving them over (small
  bent-elbow wave) — "Over here! This way!"
* **run:** Tiredness scoops up the baby; they run through the hatch, Curiosity
  bounding. Emb slams the hatch.
* **hatch / watch:** the Boss watches the empty hatch on a monitor. She does not
  call anyone. **s07_l03** "Let them run." Mystique; she lets them go.

## s08 — home (≈20 s, +4 s) — "chill"
* **dawn:** Tiredness's bedroom at dawn, soft gold light, birds outside. He
  trudges in, filthy, with Curiosity at his heels and the baby on his
  shoulder.
* **flop:** he flops face-down onto the bed (anticipation, fall, springs
  bounce, settle). His face is turned to the side on the pillow so we see it.
* **s08_l01** "Worst. Day. Ever.": muffled-ish into the pillow and slow.
* **pile:** Curiosity hops up and curls on his back; the baby climbs up and
  curls on Curiosity.
* **smile:** a close-up of his face on the pillow: a tiny real smile. Eyes close.
* **phone:** his phone on the nightstand buzzes: a text from Embarrassment,
  "you ok?". Without opening his eyes, Tiredness thumbs a reply: "zzz".
* **sleep:** wide; all three asleep, purring, sunrise light growing. ZZZ.
* **title:** end card "TIREDNESS" with subtitle "Episode 2: Lights Out", then
  "to be continued…" (captions hidden). End on a held frame.
