TIREDNESS - EPISODE 2: "LIGHTS OUT"
The creator's notes, round by round (from the first request to the latest cut)

Cuts: first cut 4:25 -> rev 2 4:51 -> rev 3 5:43 -> rev 4 (latest) 6:11
Timestamps in each round refer to the cut I was watching at the time.


THE STARTING BRIEF

Standing format (carried over from the earlier projects):
- Portrait mode for phones, under 15 MB, no black screen at the start.
- Lots of expressive face and eye movement, without overdoing it. Some of the hardest-hitting facial movements are the subtle ones.

Why Episode 2 exists:
- Episode 1 felt way too rushed. The lines went by so fast I didn't know what was going on half the time.
- Instead of fixing it, make a new episode that continues where Episode 1 left off: just Tiredness and the ally creature, having an adventure with trials and tribulations, NOT rushed.
- Keep what resonated with me: the designs of Tiredness, Embarrassment, the villain, and the ally creature.


ROUND 1: notes on the first cut (4:25)

1. Opening: make the creatures' names rude, bland or "beige". Not swear words, just flattening names like "Thing" or "It". The corporation treats these very expressive creatures as dull, drab things ("nothing to see here, go away"). It adds to the corporation's sneaky, evil energy.
2. 0:28: the corporate leader's hair covers the left side of her face, so I can't see her nose or mouth. Fix it.
3. Rename the creature (Curiosity) "Thing 1" on its pod, with the others Thing 2, Thing 3, Thing 4... I like the idea that the corporation called it Thing 1 and it turns out to be extremely emotionally expressive and intelligent.
4. 0:44, the hood gag:
   - Instead of pulling the hood down right away, the creature closes its eyes and sends a pulse outward, demonstrating: "look, I close my eyes and radiate energy to find the way."
   - Comedy: Tiredness goes "What are you doing? Are you okay?" The creature rolls its eyes and pulls the hood down.
   - Tiredness lifts the hood a little and looks with one eye, while the creature closes its eyes and pulses again.
   - A look of recognition: "Oh, it wants me to close my eyes."
5. 1:30, the hat-poking gag: put keys on the security guard and have the creature reach a paw toward them. That gives a reason for the risk instead of just irritating the guards.
6. When Tiredness says "don't do that, we could get caught", the creature rolls its eyes and stops, saying through body language: "I could've gotten you the keys, buddy."
7. 1:55 and 1:57: a blue rectangle (a bandage?) keeps appearing on the creature's face, covering it and the human's hand. Clarify it or remove it.
8. 1:44: Tiredness's arm passes in front of his face and head. Put that shoulder and arm back behind him.
9. 1:45: instead of "I'm done", something like: "I don't have the bandwidth for this right now. I'm overwhelmed. I need to take care of myself first before I can take care of others."
10. 1:59: change the "they're not my problem" energy. It IS an important problem he wants to see solved, but he doesn't see a way to help that keeps him safe and doesn't cause more violence. He needs to think about it.
11. 2:16: the creature walks down the hallway into the darkness, then magically reappears like it teleported. Make it make logical or emotional sense.
12. 2:25: more expressiveness for Tiredness. I like him super chill and nonchalant: he has an alien-looking creature behind his legs and just casually explains everything, while Embarrassment freaks out. That contrast is funny.
13. 2:41: make it clear the beeper-looking thing Embarrassment pulls out is the corporation's recording device.
14. 2:44, "my pocket calculator stopped working": add a wink or some sarcasm, so it's clear he knows exactly what he's doing. He's pausing the recorder so the corporation can't use it against his friend, and using a euphemism to cover it.
15. 2:54: Embarrassment's fingers go through the flashlight. Wrap them around it.
16. 3:02: a line at the tip of the corporate leader's nose looks like an artifact. Remove it.
17. 3:30, "I'm not tired": have him look tired while saying it, then slowly open his eyes to reveal the glow. Subtle, nothing fancy, but impressive.
18. 3:37: Tiredness's hands should actually grip the master release handle; right now they float in the air.
19. 3:43: make it clear security doors or cages slam over the pods. It looked like the pods were draining liquid.
20. 3:48: rename the freed specimen from "Joy" to a number, like "Thing 38". It shows the release freed one or two creatures before the lockdown stopped the rest.
21. 3:58: the corporate leader says something like "Oh, you too, Embarrassment. Well, no matter. I'll still catch you", in a sad, bored, emotionally numb way. Hunting down innocent lives isn't rewarding: just more numbness and going through the motions.
22. 4:03: the pizza box should look like a box, not a rectangle.
23. 4:13: Tiredness looks tired with NO smile, slowly closes his eyes, z's appear, then maybe the subtlest smile at the corner of his mouth.
24. 4:14: fix the arm that goes through his head (the arm toward the ceiling).
25. 4:16: "You okay?" becomes "You okay bro?", a term of endearment. Instead of z's, he taps the tiredness emoji, a nod to his name.

Process note (said between rounds): "I really enjoy the precise edits I can make while keeping everything else the same. That helps maintain precise emotional moments, where even a slight movement of the eye can alter the scene immensely."


ROUND 2: notes on the rev 2 cut (4:51)

1. Opening: show what the creature is doing. Thing 1 looks into a pod at a smaller sibling resting inside, with a look of "maybe we can get them out of there". Then Thing 1 drops down into the normal scene next to Tiredness.
2. Line change: "They're not things" becomes "They're not JUST things". Technically they exist, but they're not a bland object like a rock. They have a complex lived experience, with emotional and social intelligence; not something to lock in a cage.
3. 0:52: when Thing 1 closes its eyes, it does so FORCEFULLY, with visible creases, communicating "you gotta close your eyes, bro". Hold it for a second or two so the viewer gets it.
4. 1:10: Thing 1 SIGHS (relief) instead of rolling its eyes. Tiredness IS listening, even if he downplays it; the sigh says "at least he's learning something."
5. 1:36: the key tag changes from "PODS" to "TOTALLY NOT THE POD KEYS". It's comedy, and it explains why Thing 1 goes for the keys. I want a mix of emotional energy: some seriousness, some comedy, some sadness, some irritation at how the corporation behaves.
6. 2:05: add a Tiredness line after "no bandwidth": "...but I don't want to abandon you." A poignant moment: he's protecting his own well-being, but he won't abandon the creature.
7. 2:15: "Maybe me" becomes "Maybe even me", slowed down a touch. He's processing that his life could be on the line.
8. 2:27, after the groan, work in both ideas:
   - Tiredness: "Okay. I'll help. Hell or high water. I hope we make it out of this", with a sad glance at the creature.
   - Then Embarrassment yells off-screen, terrified: "Oh my God, what IS this thing?!" Tiredness sighs and gets up: he can't let the creature get hurt, or let Embarrassment panic.
9. 2:33: Tiredness's right hand (left side of the screen) goes missing. Fix it.
10. 2:40: instead of just "Oh, hey", Tiredness explains: "Oh, hey. Yeah. I'm helping my creature take down a massive, horrible corporation doing shady things." Embarrassment gives an awkward laugh with a hand behind his head ("oh no, that's my employer").
11. Embarrassment doesn't know the name "Thing One", so instead he says: "Is that one of the creatures from the lab I work at? You shouldn't be around that. We should bring it back."
12. 2:57 (and throughout): the band-aid below Tiredness's nose sticks out off his face. Make it sit flat on his skin.
13. After Embarrassment says to bring it back, Tiredness: "Buddy. Your company is locking these creatures up. That's not cool. We gotta save them. Okay, dude?"
14. 3:32: the corporate leader's lip outline is too thick; it looks like black lipstick. Make it thinner.
15. Line change: "for once... I'm not tired" becomes "But right now... I'm not tired." He's tired most of the time and that's okay, it's who he is. Just not right now, because the creatures deserve freedom and to thrive.
16. 4:30: a little sigh from the corporate leader, eyes looking down, lids mostly closed. It hints at what's going on underneath: a mystery.
17. 4:40: remove the sweat beads in bed. There's no reason for him to sweat.
18. 4:45: change the emoji to the sleepy one with the z's.
19. Rename the corporation "CalmCorp" everywhere, to match Part 1 from my other project.

3. 0:33: remove the sweat drips from Tiredness; he's not doing anything physically intense.

6. 2:28: remove the sweat from Tiredness again.
7. 2:57: Thing 1 walks down the hallway with its eyes shining gently in the dark, so we see Thing 1 and Embarrassment interact before Tiredness stands up.
   - Right now Thing 1 just stares.
   - Tiredness doesn't want to leave Thing 1 all by itself, he wants to help.
   - The final push that gets him to stand up is Embarrassment seeing Thing 1 and freaking out.
8. 3:37: "A critter bit me" becomes "A creature bit me". It wasn't a bug; it's a more complex animal.
9. 3:56: when Embarrassment pauses the recorder, he says "Oh, look at that. My recorder just stopped working." That makes more sense than "Huh."
10. Throughout: the inside of Tiredness's and the corporate leader's mouths is very dark, like a blob moving around. Brighten it a little so you can see how their mouths move when they talk.
11. "I'm not tired" becomes "I'm not..." [pause] "...really all that tired right now", with his eyes growing big and maybe a pulse of glow as he says it. More energy right before he pulls the lever.
12. After her sigh, the corporate leader gets up slowly and tiredly, a touch of comedy:
   - her hands on the armrests, getting up sluggishly, with an "oomph" and her knees cracking;

   - Her energy: she's just tired. "Do I really want to be chasing down someone who's trying to save creatures from being experimented on? Is this really what I'm doing with my life?" She has to wear the villain mask, or she'd be fired, ostracized, hurt, or thrown in a jail cell.
13. 5:35: remove the subtle smile. Tiredness is too exhausted to smile at all. The lightness comes from sending the sleeping emoji; I don't want two forms of lightness in that beat.
   - No smiles anywhere in the ending scene where he's sleeping or getting ready for bed.
