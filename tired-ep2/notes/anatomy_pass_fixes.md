TIREDNESS - EPISODE 2: ANATOMY & GLITCH PASS (after rev 4)
What the checklist passes found and fixed. Times are in the final cut. No dialogue, timing or acting was changed; every fix is local, and the rest of each scene was verified pixel-identical.

HOW IT WAS CHECKED
- Every character's head, hands, elbows, shoulders, feet and arm layering were recorded on every frame of the film (8,896 frames). The data was scanned for one- and two-frame twitches, snaps/teleports and z-order flicker.
- One reviewer per scene then went shot by shot through the checklist:
  - motion;
  - hands and held objects;
  - arms and shoulders;
  - neck, head, face and hair;
  - legs and feet;
  - liveliness and light.

WHOLE FILM (rig)
- Walking: the body dropped for one frame on every step (5-12 px at 720p in closer shots). Now each step settles smoothly. Walk speeds are unchanged.
- Creature head turns through a front view: the eyes, nose, mouth, ear fins and head tilt used to jump to the other side in one frame. The head now swings through a centred front view, with the far ear coming round. All other head angles are unchanged.
  The 14 turns that crossed the front view in a single frame were also eased so they pass through it smoothly:
  - s01: 0:05.5, 0:10.4, 0:14.8
  - s03: 1:34.2
  - s04: 2:17.2 (close-up), 2:19.5, 2:21.3, 2:22.2, 2:44.1, 2:45.0, 2:51.1, 2:51.9
  - s05: 3:15.2, 3:23.1
  Starts, ends and all other acting are unchanged.
- Creature tears: now light blue instead of white.

FINAL RE-SCAN
After the fixes, every frame was re-scanned. What remains flagged is deliberate:
- impact camera shakes and Emb's terrified trembles;
- the bonk;
- the scared guard's startle jolt;
- fast runs;
- one creature hop-turn at the top of a hop;
- one whole-body turn-around.

CHECKED AND ALREADY FINE
- Hands: no blobs, and no ovals inside them.
- Necks and heads: no stray lines and no floating heads; necks are a natural length.
- Shoulders: no mud-like shading.
- Hair and hoods: hair attached, hoods sitting on the head.
- Gaze: eye lines on the person being addressed.
- Feet and seated poses: feet point the way the body faces, and seated poses are possible.
- Light and props: nobody unnaturally dark, props resting on surfaces.
- Tears: never vanish in pieces.
- Motion: impact camera shakes (0.6 s or less) and the characters' deliberate trembles were left as they are.
