# Production Bible — "The Void Has a Sense of Humor" (3D)

A portrait (9:16, **720×1280, 24 fps**) **3D** animated short with voice acting, narration, an original score,
sound effects and burned-in subtitles, built entirely in code: three.js rendered frame by frame in headless
Chromium (SwiftShader WebGL), Kokoro neural TTS for voices, Whisper word alignment for lip-sync, a
FluidR3 GM soundfont + numpy synthesis for music and SFX, ffmpeg/libass for subtitles, mux and encode.

This is a companion piece to the creator's earlier 2D short **"The Void and My Fear"** (same Void, same Fear,
same narrator voice). The bonus scene happens *during* that film's cage scene.

## The creator's brief (summary of their requirements, all binding)

- **3D, in the visual spirit of a luminous Pixar-style "inside the mind" film** (glowing, effervescent emotion
  beings, saturated pastel light, big expressive eyes) — **but 100% original**: no character, set, prop,
  logo, name or design from any existing film or brand. Colours are fine; shapes/designs must be our own.
- Portrait mode for phones. As long as needed for a coherent narrative (expect ~4:30–6:30).
- Compress hard: target **< 15 MB** total; slight quality loss is OK if it stays coherent and readable.
- **Voice acting + narration + background music** to deepen emotional resonance with viewers' lived experience.
- The story text is a GUIDE: use its words directly when they work, be creative where it helps.
- **PRIORITY: facial movements (eyes, eyelids, brows, mouth), body language, emotionally in-depth dialogue,
  and characters reacting to dialogue with nuance and precision.** The creator prefers conversation and
  narration over wild, outlandish animation. Expressions should be alive but not excessive.
- **First frame IS the video** (no fade in from black): frame 0 must be a finished, legible, inviting,
  bright-enough image (it's the social-media preview still). A title may sit on frame 0 and fade OUT.
- **Subtitles** burned in (and an .srt sidecar).
- **Musical word-painting**: on many (not all) lines, a **high note** (celesta/glock/harp/flute) lands on
  hopeful / light / funny words and a **low note** (contrabass/cello/timpani/low piano) on existential /
  sad / heavy words — enough to feel the swells, not so many it distracts. Marked in the screenplay text
  as `^word` (high) and `~word` (low).
- **Say it, show it**: when a sentence describes an action, that action is visibly happening on screen at
  that moment (if the line says "coughing up stars and galaxies", stars and galaxies are coming out).
- No names of copyrighted characters anywhere. (Real people/brands as words in dialogue, like "Twitter",
  are fine; **no logos**: the phone app is a generic social app with a plain speech-bubble icon.)
- Profanity: "what the actual f***" is bleeped in audio (a comedic bleep) and written `f***` in subtitles.
- Experimental/surprising choices are welcome; the creator will request changes afterward.

### Required story beats (all must appear, in this order)
PART ONE
1. A Twitter user is crying and running from the Void. Jesus appears, now, and sees this.
2. Jesus walks up to the Void, puts his arm around it, whispers in its ear.
3. The Void laughs maniacally, coughing up stars and galaxies from laughing so hard.
4. Jesus looks at the Twitter user — on their backside, one hand up, terror in their eyes — and says:
   "The void's got a pretty good sense of humor. Teehee."
5. Twitter user: "What the actual f***" — as the Void finishes laughing.
6. The Void whispers into Jesus's ear while side-eyeing the user; Jesus nods along.
7. The Void crosses its arms and scowls at the user. Jesus: "The void says it wants to talk to you about
   something important. Do you have a moment?"
8. User: "Hell no, I'm not talking to that scary thing."
9. The Void rolls its eyes, huffs and puffs, turns its back to the user.
10. Jesus's awkward "oh boy, this is awkward" face (clenched-teeth smile, eyes darting, brows up).
11. Jesus puts a hand on the Void's shoulder; the Void looks over its shoulder and gives Jesus a gentle
    smile; Jesus nods slightly.
12. Jesus turns back to the user: it's okay, you don't have to talk to the void — but what about talking to
    me? Not about the void; about you, the emotions you're feeling. I want to talk with you more about
    emotions, because that's meaningful to me. How does that sound?
13. The user gets up, storms off, and blocks Jesus on Twitter (for talking about the void too intensely).
14. Jesus looks back at the Void and shrugs. The Void pats Jesus on the shoulder with a sad smile.
15. Jesus puts his arm around the Void; they walk off toward the horizon, Jesus smiling a bit, telling more
    dumb jokes, the Void slightly giggling in the distance.
PART TWO — BONUS
16. Narrator (first person) imagines the moment the Void opened the cage to "my fear".
17. The Depression Magician lurks right behind the Void's big body, dagger and numbing dust ready, licking
    its lips: "Fear looks very juicy. Can't wait to knock Fear out."
18. The Void sees the magician in the corner of its peripheral vision (eyes slide, head does NOT turn —
    it keeps calm for Fear's sake). No shouting, no fuss.
19. The Void kicks the magician in the chest with its fat leg; the magician flies and crashes into buckets
    and cleaning supplies, slapstick-style.
20. Fear: "What's that noise?" The Void shrugs, innocent.
21. The Void goes on showing Fear the galaxies and nebulae — the whole point: so the Void could have a
    moment with my fear. (Wonder. Tears of awe. Warmth.)

## Cast (original designs)

Proportions are stylized (big heads, big eyes). Heights in metres of the 3D world (feet on ground y=0).

- **NARRATOR** (voice-over only) — the storyteller, the "I". Playful, warm, a little wry, tender when it
  matters. Same voice as the earlier film (50/50 blend of two Kokoro voices, gender-neutral).
- **JESUS** — height 1.75. Warm, kind, playful, unhurried, a gentle tease; utterly unafraid. Stylized human:
  head ~1/4 of height, shoulder-length wavy dark-brown hair parted in the middle, short full beard, warm
  olive-tan skin, large warm brown eyes, thick expressive brows, laugh lines. Cream/oatmeal homespun robe
  (long, simple), a terracotta-red sash/mantle draped over one shoulder, a rope belt, brown sandals. A very
  subtle warm golden rim glow (no halo disc, no sacred-heart iconography). Body language: open palms, easy
  shoulders, leans in to listen, kneels to people's level.
- **USER** ("the Twitter user") — height 1.65. Young adult, gender-ambiguous-to-feminine, voice young and
  quick (sarcastic when scared). Oversized slate-blue hoodie (strings, kangaroo pocket), dark joggers,
  chunky white sneakers, messy dark-brown hair in a short bob with a cowlick, big round eyes, freckles,
  slightly too-big round glasses optional. Always holding a phone (screen glows cool white on their face).
  Body language: hunched, defensive, phone as a shield; terror → disbelief → refusal → a flicker of being
  seen → walls back up.
- **THE VOID** — height ~3.0 (towering but round and plump: wider than tall at the base). The same Void as
  the earlier film, now in 3D and finally *expressive*: a huge, soft, hooded, cloaked presence of deep
  indigo-black velvet, with faint stars drifting *inside* the fabric and a violet→teal rim glow (it is an
  inner being: effervescent edge, tiny star motes occasionally lift off it). Its hood opening is a field of
  deep cosmic darkness where **two large soft starlight eyes** (moon-white, slightly teal irises of light)
  live, with eyelids of darkness that can scowl, droop, side-eye and roll; **wisps of nebula light act as
  brows**; a **mouth** appears as a crescent opening into deeper cosmos (stars visible inside) when it
  smiles, scowls or laughs. Big gentle four-fingered hands (dark velvet, starlit edges) from wide sleeves.
  **Short, fat, round legs** with rounded feet peek out from the hem when it walks (and kicks). It never
  says words: only whispers (we never understand them), laughs, huffs, giggles, hums.
- **FEAR** (bonus) — the creator's Fear from the earlier film: small (0.85 tall), soft pale-lavender
  bean/teardrop body, oversized round eyes with big irises and heavy expressive upper lids, thin worried
  brows, a wobbly antenna-tuft, thin arms, knobbly little legs, a chunky hand-knitted mustard scarf.
  Effervescent glow. Trembles when scared. Voice small, breathy, quick to crack. (Not lanky, no bow tie,
  no single curl — it is a little bean with a scarf.)
- **THE DEPRESSION MAGICIAN** (bonus) — height 2.1 but stooped. A creepy-funny stage magician made of
  desaturated grey-blue gloom: dented tall top hat, long tattered tailcoat with a high collar, white gloves
  gone grey, a gaunt pale-grey face with heavy-lidded sunken eyes, a too-wide thin grin, a thin pencil
  moustache. Holds a wavy little dagger in one hand and a velvet pouch of **numbing dust** (grey glitter that
  drains colour where it falls) in the other. Leaves a faint grey dust trail. Theatrical, slithery voice.
  Slapstick-durable: gets kicked, flies, crashes, bucket on head, mop on head, little circling stars.

## Locations

- **THE_EDGE** (Part One) — an original luminous dreamscape at golden dusk: soft rolling pastel hills
  (peach, lilac, mint), a pale winding path leading to a far horizon with a huge low warm sun/glow; fluffy
  backlit clouds; scattered glowing dandelion-like "thought seeds" drifting slowly; a few soft crystal-ish
  rounded trees. Where the Void stands, the sky above deepens to indigo with stars (its presence). Ending:
  the path into the sunset/horizon.
- **MIND_BACKROOM** (Bonus) — the back of the creator's mind, as in the earlier film: a dim, cavernous, cozy
  back room. The little **time-out cage** (birdcage-like, a small hanging "TIME-OUT" tag) sits here; in a
  corner a **janitor's corner**: metal buckets, a mop in a wringer bucket, brooms, spray bottles, a stack of
  rags, a yellow wet-floor sign (generic, no brand). Soft cool light from far away (the screen of
  consciousness glow), the Void's starlight. When the Void shows Fear the cosmos, nebulae and galaxies fill
  the whole space around them.

## Look & style rules (the "luminous mind-film" look, original)

- Soft, saturated, light-filled; ACES tone mapping; bloom on glowing elements; warm key / cool fill.
- Inner beings (Void, Fear, Magician) have **effervescent** materials: soft subsurface-like wrap shading,
  fresnel rim glow, a fine slow-moving surface shimmer (low contrast — compression-friendly), sparse motes.
- Humans (Jesus, User) are stylized Pixar-like: smooth skin, big eyes with catchlights, soft rim light.
- **Eyes are the soul**: large irises, crisp pupils, wet catchlights, eyelids that actually move (blinks,
  half-lids, squints, droops), brows with independent inner/outer control, saccades and focus shifts.
- Mouths: clear lip-sync from audio (visemes), plus smiles, frowns, pressed lips, clenched-teeth grimace,
  trembling lip, open laugh.
- Camera: mostly gentle (slow push-ins, subtle drift, cut on action). Portrait framing.
- **Frame layout**: subtitles live in the band **y ≈ 880–1060 px** (of 1280). Keep faces and key action in
  **y ≈ 110–850**. Top ~90 px and bottom ~200 px may be covered by social-media UI.
- Compression: avoid full-screen high-frequency noise/flicker, keep particles sparse and slow, avoid huge
  sudden full-frame flashes; prefer smooth gradients and clean shapes.

## Technical constraints (for writers)

- 3D, 24 fps, 720×1280. Camera can cut, push, pull, pan, orbit slightly, rack focus (simulated), dissolve.
- ≤ 4 characters on screen. Lip-sync for JESUS, USER, FEAR, MAGICIAN. NARRATOR is voice-over only.
- The Void's vocalizations are `vocal` beats (laugh, giggle, whisper, huff, hum, sigh, snort, cough).
- Speech pacing: natural to slightly slow (~140–155 wpm), with real pauses so faces can act.
