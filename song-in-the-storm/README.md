# The Song in the Storm

A two-part animated short, inspired by the feeling of listening to a beautiful song in a world that abandons the people who sing it.

- `part1_the_storm.mp4`: Part One, *The Storm* (3:06)
- `part2_the_spark.mp4`: Part Two, *The Spark* (3:42)

## Story

**Part One.** A worn-out man walks home through the rain after a double shift and ducks into an old station waiting room. A homeless young woman is singing there. Her song moves him to tears, then pulls up memories of a society that looks away: waitlists, eviction notices, spiked benches, "if you help them, who helps you?" A storm builds in his mind. Horns grow, and his eyes turn red.

**Part Two.** Deep in the storm, a tiny point of light appears. It is faint, yet far brighter than anything around it, and lightning can't put it out. His horns crack and crumble into embers. The light enters his chest and he takes on the likeness of Jesus. Back in the waiting room he kneels in front of her, tells her she was never the problem, and gives her his mantle and his dinner. Then he walks out into the night carrying the spark, and windows across the city light up one by one.

## The song

"Little Light" is original: the melody, lyrics and chords are all new and were written for this film. It does not use any part of *My Dearest*. The vocal is synthesized, so expect it to sound somewhat artificial.

## How it's made

Everything is generated by code, with no stock assets.

- `audio/story.py`: the script, every dialogue timing, and the song (notes and chords).
- `audio/build_audio.py`: voices from Kokoro TTS. The "demon" voice is pitch-shifted with the WORLD vocoder, and society's voices are echoed. The singing turns TTS words into melody by retiming them with WORLD. Score and SFX come from `synth.py` (piano, strings, choir, bells, rain, thunder, wind, heartbeat). It also writes `partN_timeline.json`, which holds the captions and per-frame mouth envelopes used for lip sync.
- `animation/`: an HTML canvas renderer.
  - `character.js` is the face rig: gaze, blinks, brows, lids, tears, mouth shapes, horns, red eyes, halo, and a hair/beard/robe morph into the Christ persona.
  - `figure.js` draws the full-body figures.
  - `env.js` draws the city, station, storm mindscape and memory vignettes.
  - `scenes.js` and `scenes2.js` hold the shot lists for each part.
  - `render.js` and `render_all.sh` capture frames headlessly at 24 fps and encode them with ffmpeg.

Each shot in `scenes*.js` is a small function of time. To change an expression, a camera move or a moment, edit that shot. To change a line, edit `story.py`, then rebuild the audio and re-render.
