Here's a conscious zombie story.

I had an idea the other day where I saw a zombie, and its brothers and sisters were zombies as well, and they were running towards the Fortress of Humanity. And Humanity was defending itself by tearing those zombies into dust, to protect themselves from the scariness of the mindless zombies.

But then I saw a soul descend from the heavens, and it fell into one of the zombies. The eyes of the zombie opened up, realizing that it could witness, and then that zombie started looking around, and it froze in place. The conscious zombie realized it had control of itself, but it did not have control of what it could witness and what it could feel. Its brothers and sisters, who were unconscious and looked just like it, seemed compelled to run to the battlefield. It saw them getting destroyed by the Fortress of Humanity, and it saw their suffering, even though they were not conscious. And then the pain started, because it could feel their pain now, all of their pain. Now that this soul-infused zombie had become conscious, it was a witness to the suffering in the world.

And the conscious zombie thought it was alone in its suffering in the world. It fell to its knees, and it covered its face to try to suppress the pain it had witnessed. It had no other consciousness to share that suffering with, as far as it knew, so it was maybe the only one giving the suffering around it meaning.

Because before that body of flesh was conscious, the suffering had no meaning. But when a soul descended from heaven and created a witness, the suffering had meaning. And now that body was wondering why. Why was it picked to witness the suffering, and what did that mean? What power is there to give meaning to suffering, to be on your knees and to have tears fall from your eyes as you witness others who cannot share it with you? But you are giving their suffering meaning, by seeing their pain.

Now that body of flesh sees the suffering and wonders why it exists. But there is no answer from the heavens, because the heavens gave their answer when they sent the soul and put it into a body of flesh. And now that body of flesh needs to find its own answer to what the suffering means. Maybe heaven's answer and heaven's question were the same thing, which was your existence. And maybe if they knew more, they would have already solved it. But your own ability to witness on a deep level might give you the details you need to help them.

What if heaven sent the soul that awakened the zombie into witnessing a kind of hell, not for the soul to take the highway towards hell, but to better know what to avoid? Because the staircase to heaven is not a simple staircase. It is built by transforming the suffering of hell into well-being and peace.

And then the conscious zombie had cried all of the tears that it could, and it looked around and saw a building. The conscious zombie climbed up a ladder to the top of the building, and it saw a flag. It grabbed the flag and started waving it in the air, because it had to do something. It couldn't watch all of its brothers and sisters die. The pain was unbearable. The suffering was unbearable.

And then a human helicopter came flying over. A rope came out of the helicopter, and one of the humans dropped down the rope, grabbed the conscious zombie by the shirt, and pulled them up into the helicopter. The conscious zombie thought it was going to get shot or killed like the other zombies. But then the zombie saw that the humans were looking at it.

The humans said, "Who are you?"
The conscious zombie said, "I don't know. I am a zombie, I think."
The humans looked at each other, raised their eyebrows, and said, "We think you are conscious."
The conscious zombie said, "How can you tell?"
And the humans said, "Because you can feel the pain of your brothers and sisters, and you can feel the weight of the world. That's what humans feel, and we think you feel that too."
And the conscious zombie nodded.

Without hell, which was what existed before heaven, heaven could not have been formed. And that is why heaven sent the soul: to turn hell into heaven.
