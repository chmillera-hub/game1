# ANGER — production bible

A ~3:53 portrait (720×1280, 24 fps) animated short. Final file: H.264 + AAC MP4, ≤ 14 MB, burned-in
subtitles + .srt. Everything generated by code in this folder (Kokoro TTS, FluidSynth/numpy music,
numpy SFX, skia-python animation) with the same pipeline as `../if-you-have-time`.

**Original IP only.** "The friend" has the *energy* of a huge sleepy round creature but must NOT copy
any existing character (no Snorlax colours, face mask, shape language or name). No Pokémon, no Inside Out.

**The film never starts on black.** Frame 0 already shows the torch flame lighting Anger's face.

## 1. Story in one breath
ANGER — a huge, grim, armored warrior — advances through a cave by torchlight: he hacks through vines,
swipes webs aside with his gauntlet, finds a locked door, backs up, charges, and the door explodes open
onto a vast cavern. While he searches it, glowing eyes blink in a crevice and scurry off. A stone shifts,
his torch flies into a pool — darkness. Dead silence; something scurries behind him; he looks — nothing.
He presses on; the floor collapses; he hangs from the ledge, too heavy, slipping… and falls, stoic,
accepting it — until he rams his sword into the wall, sparks screaming, slowing him just enough to
survive the impact. Barely conscious at the bottom, he can move one arm. The glowing-eyed crawler comes
for him — drooling — and right before it bites, a thrown rock knocks it out. A voice in the dark:
"You gotta watch out for those things…" A leather glove drags the monster off; crunching. Anger props
himself against the wall: "Who are you?" — "…now a nap sounds even better." Snoring. Anger questions his
life choices, gives up, sleeps. Drip… drip… on his cheek: a HUGE round creature's tongue, beady eyes,
dumb grin. "What the hell are you?" — "That's my friend… I think he likes you." Sass, stomps, an awkward
standoff, a ridiculous laugh, "now you're stuck in here with us — how do you feel about that?" —
"Well… I guess I'll die then." The creature snorts and giggles; Anger smirks, catches himself, clears
his throat — dead serious again.

Tone: an epic, genuinely tense first half, played completely straight (the joke is that he IS that
serious), sliding into deadpan comedy at the bottom. Faces: subtle, precise micro-expressions — the
hardest-hitting moments are small (an eye roll, one raised eyebrow, a smirk suppressed).

## 2. Characters

### ANGER (am_onyx, slow and gravelly)
- Huge: HEIGHT ≈ 820 stage units at scale 1. Massive shoulders and chest, thick arms, slightly small
  head for heroic proportions. Shaved head, heavy brow ridge, deep-set dark eyes, thick dark beard, broad
  nose, a pale scar through the left eyebrow. Default face: grim, utterly serious.
- Armor: weathered steel plate (`a_steel` + shade/highlight) — big pauldrons, breastplate with an
  ORIGINAL emblem (a simple stylized flame/claw mark), gauntlets, greaves; leather straps and belt
  (`a_leather`), a torn dark-red tabard (`a_cloth`), small gold rivets (`a_gold`).
- Gear: broadsword (right hand), round iron-rimmed shield on his back, torch (left hand) until it's lost.
- Rig: `anim/char_anger.py`, same contract as the other films (draw/head_center/hand_pos/ARMS/HEIGHT;
  Pose fields for eyes/brows/mouth/lip-sync/blink/breath/light/tint/rim) plus `pose.extra` keys, at least:
  - `state`: "stand", "walk", "run", "chop", "swipe", "door_push", "charge", "stumble", "hang",
    "fall", "sword_drag", "crumpled", "lie_back", "prop_sit", "sleep" (blendable where sensible)
  - `phase` (0..1 or cycles) for walk/run/chop/swipe/door; `torch` (bool / which hand), `sword`
    ("hand", "sheathed", "in_wall", None), `shield` ("back", "arm"), `cross_arms` (0..1),
    `one_eye` (0..1 — open one eye only), `smirk_suppress`, `bruised` (0..1 dust/scrapes after the fall)
  - walk cadence is FIXED: 1.10 s per walk cycle (a heavy footfall every 0.55 s); run 0.62 s per cycle.
    The rig exports `STEP_TIMES(state, t0, t1)` or equivalent so scenes can report foot plants.

### THE CRAWLER (the monster)
- Wolf-sized, lean and low: hunched ridged spine, long whip tail, four long-clawed legs, slick dark
  hide (`m_skin`), FOUR small glowing yellow-green eyes (`m_eye`, emissive — they read in total darkness),
  wide jaw full of needle teeth (`m_tooth`, `m_gum`), long tongue, strands of drool (`m_drool`).
- Rig: `anim/char_crawler.py`: states "lurk" (eyes only in a crevice), "scurry" (fast low run), "creep",
  "snarl" (jaw open, drool, tongue), "lunge", "ko" (knocked out: limp, eyes dim, tongue out), "dragged"
  (pulled by the tail). Eyes must be drawable as an emissive pass after the darkness overlay.

### THE FRIEND (non-verbal: huffs, snorts, giggles)
- ENORMOUS (≈ 2× Anger's height), round pear-shaped body, shaggy warm-brown fur (`b_fur`), cream belly
  (`b_belly`), mossy patches, two small curled horn nubs, tiny round ears, tiny beady black eyes, a wide
  dumb grin with big flat teeth, a long pink tongue (`b_tongue`) that drips saliva. Short stubby arms that
  CAN cross over the belly; stubby legs that stomp (the ground shakes). Original design.
- Rig: `anim/char_friend.py`: expressions: dumb_grin, side_eye, squint, huff, snort, giggle (paws over
  mouth, shoulders bouncing), smug; arms: rest, cross, cover_mouth, tap; legs: stand, stomp_back (sassy
  backward stomps), foot_tap; tongue out/dripping.

### THE VOICE (am_santa) — the hermit in the dark
- Never seen. Only: a voice from the darkness at screen-left, and once a weathered LEATHER GLOVE
  (`glove`) with a ragged sleeve that reaches out of the dark, grabs the crawler's tail and drags it away.
  Draw helper `draw_glove(canvas, x, y, scale, grip, angle)` (in `anim/char_crawler.py` or fx).

## 3. Sets (`anim/env.py`) and light (`anim/light.py`)
- **Tunnel (S1):** a rough rock passage receding into the dark; hanging roots/vines (cuttable: they fall
  in pieces), spider webs between rocks (tearable), loose stones, the DOOR at the end: tall old wooden
  planks bound with iron, set in a carved stone arch; states: closed, rattling, EXPLODING (planks and
  splinters flying, dust cloud, a burst of cold air), broken frame afterwards.
- **Cavern (S2):** vast; high ceiling with stalactites, rock pillars receding, a dark CREVICE in a wall
  (where the eyes appear), a still POOL of black water (where the torch lands: splash, steam, ripples),
  scattered stones (one shifts), patches of faint teal bioluminescent fungi, a far archway (the other door).
- **The fall (S3):** the floor collapsing into a hole (stones dropping); the ledge he hangs from; a
  vertical rock-and-soil shaft wall rushing past during the fall; the sword gouge with a stream of
  sparks and dirt; blackness below; the impact (dust burst).
- **The depths (S4–S6):** the bottom grotto: rubble and dust, a rock wall to prop against, roots, a few
  dim fungi patches, deep shadows on screen-left where the voice is; later the friend fills the frame.
- **Light:** everything is drawn lit, then `light.py` applies DARKNESS with light holes: torch (warm,
  flickering, moves with Anger's hand), fungi (very dim teal), sparks (brief warm bursts), the door
  explosion flash. After the torch dies the world is near-dark but READABLE (dim teal ambient + a cold rim
  on Anger). Emissive items (crawler eyes, sparks, embers) are drawn after the darkness. Compression:
  smooth dark gradients, no grain; light falloff smooth.

## 4. Shot-by-shot direction (beat names from build/timeline.json; never hard-code seconds)
### S1 — The approach (0 – 36.5)
- Frame 0: CLOSE on the torch flame, Anger's grim face half-lit beside it (NOT black). Slow pull back.
  `title_in`…`title_out`: "ANGER" (heavy, wide-tracked, ember-orange glow) over the shot.
- `walk_start`: low-angle hero shots as he strides down the tunnel, torch high, sword low, shield on back.
- `vines`: a curtain of hanging vines blocks the way; `chop1`/`chop2`/`chop3`: three heavy sword chops,
  vines falling in pieces.
- `webs`: thick webs; `web_swipe1`/`web_swipe2`: back-hand swipes with his gauntlet tearing them away.
- `door_arrive`: the door. `door_try1`/`door_try2`: he grips the iron ring and pulls/pushes — locked, it
  rattles. A flat look. `back_up`: he steps back several paces, rolls his shoulders, lowers his head.
  `charge`: he runs at it, shoulder first; `door_burst`: the door EXPLODES open (splinters, dust, flash,
  camera shake). Dust settles into…
### S2 — The cavern (36.5 – 76.5)
- `reveal`: he steps through the broken door into a VAST cavern (wide, he is tiny; torch light pool).
  `look_around`: slow pans, his eyes scanning. `search_start`: he searches (walks, checks behind rocks).
- `eyes_glow`: in the background, four glowing eyes blink in a crevice … `eyes_scurry`: they skitter away
  (he doesn't see it — the audience does).
- `stone_shift`: a stone tilts under his foot, he stumbles; `torch_fly`: the torch tumbles end over end
  through the air; `torch_splash`: into the pool — hiss, steam; `torch_out`: DARK.
- `listen`: dead silence (only drips). He stands still, eyes moving, listening. `scurry_behind`: a shape
  scuttles across behind him; `over_shoulder`: he looks over his shoulder — nothing.
- `continue`: he moves on cautiously toward the far archway, sword raised.
### S3 — The fall (76.5 – 96.5)
- `wrong_rock`: a crack; `collapse`: the floor gives way; `grab_ledge`: one gauntlet catches the edge, he
  dangles, sword in the other hand; `slipping`: fingers sliding on dusty stone, dust trickling, armor
  dragging him down. His face: strain, then calm. `let_go`/`falling`: he drops into blackness — NO scream;
  close on his face: stoic acceptance, eyes closing slowly. `sword_thrust`: his eyes snap open — he rams
  the sword into the wall with both hands: a fountain of sparks and soil, the blade carving a long gouge,
  his fall slowing; `impact`: he hits the bottom hard (dust burst, camera jolt), then stillness.
### S4 — The depths (96.5 – 140.3)
- `dazed`: dim, blurred, doubled vision (vignette); a groan. `assess`: he tries to move — fingers, a wince;
  his eyes look around: shadows. `eyes_again`: the glowing eyes and a scurrying shadow. `pass_out`: his eyes
  close; the frame dims (not to black).
- `wake_noise`: pebbles shift; his eyes open. `one_arm`: on his back he can barely move — only one arm.
- `monster_approach`: the eyes approach out of the dark, low growl; `arm_up`: he raises his one arm weakly;
  `monster_reveal`: the crawler's head looms: needle teeth, dripping drool, tongue hanging; `monster_lunge`:
  it lunges — `rock_hit`: a rock flies in and CLONKS it on the head; it drops, KO.
- `one_eye_open`: Anger opens ONE eye: the monster is out cold. Confusion.
- m01/m02 from the darkness (screen-left). `glove_grab`: a leather glove reaches out of the dark, grabs the
  tail, drags it away into darkness; `crunch`: crunching sounds. Anger's face: no idea how to react.
### S5 — The voice (140.3 – 164.0)
- `prop_up`: he shifts back and props himself against the wall, legs out. a01 "Who are you? How did you get
  here?" — silence — m03 — `eye_roll`: Anger rolls his eyes. `snore_start`: loud snoring from the dark.
  `contemplate`: he stares into nothing, questioning his life choices; a02 (muttered) — a long breath —
  `close_eyes`: he gives up and closes his eyes.
### S6 — The friend (164.0 – 233.2)
- `sleeping`: head tilted, asleep, snoring continues. `drip1`/`drip2`: drips land on his cheek;
  `hand_wave`: he swats; `side_eye_open`: one exhausted eye opens, looking up sideways; `sees_tongue`: a huge
  pink tongue dripping right above his face; `startled`: he jolts awake — the FRIEND looms over him,
  dumb grin, beady eyes.
- a03 "What the hell are you?" — m04 — `shift_away`: he shifts out from under the drips, squinting —
  a04 — `sass`: the friend side-eyes him, crosses its arms and stomps backward with sass (three stomps,
  the ground shakes, dust falls). m05 — `wipe_saliva`: Anger rolls his eyes and wipes his face.
- `awkward`: awkward silence: the friend squints, arms crossed, tapping its foot; Anger looks off into the
  distance pretending not to notice. a05 — `laugh_start` m06: the voice laughs for way too long; Anger
  raises one eyebrow and crosses his arms. m07. a06 "Well… I guess I'll die then."
- `snort`: the friend snorts and covers its mouth, giggling. `anger_smirk`: the tiniest smirk on Anger;
  `notices_smirk`: he notices, clears his throat (sfx) — `serious`: dead serious again.
- `end_card`: "ANGER" title over the dark grotto (the friend's silhouette maybe still giggling), fade out
  over the last ~1 s.

## 5. Music (`audio/music.py`) — cue lengths in script_data.MUSIC_CUES; hit points = timeline beats
Anger's theme: a heavy, heroic minor motif (D minor), low brass + taiko, e.g. D–A–F–E | D–C–D (original).
- `descent` (0 → `reveal_settle`): ominous epic: low string ostinato, taiko pulses on his strides, brass
  swells, wordless low choir; builds through vines/webs; a tense pause at the locked door, a run-up roll at
  `charge`, a massive HIT on `door_burst`, then awe as the dust settles.
- `cavern` (S2 start → `torch_splash`): vast mystery: wide low pads, distant choir, harp/celesta glints, a
  sting at `eyes_glow`; cuts off dead at the splash.
- (silence + drips) → `tension` (`continue` → `collapse`): a low heartbeat pulse + tremolo.
- `fall` (`collapse` → `fall_end`): a hit at the collapse, straining strings while he hangs, then while he
  falls a solemn sustained choir chord (time stops), a violent brass/percussion surge at `sword_thrust`
  with screeching strings, a final thunderous hit at `impact`, then nothing.
- `depths` (S4 start → `monster_approach`): low, aching drones, faint heartbeat.
- `menace` (`monster_approach` → `rock_hit`): creeping crescendo, cut dead at the rock hit.
- `hermit` (`hermit_start` → `crunch_end`): a quirky little bassoon/pizzicato figure (the comedy starts).
- `lull` (`snore_start` → `startled`): a soft, slightly absurd lullaby under the snoring.
- `friend` (`startled` → `end_card`): comedic: tuba/bassoon/pizzicato waddle theme for the friend, a sassy
  stomp figure at `sass`, drops out for the `awkward` silence (just foot taps), a deadpan sting at a06,
  a bouncy giggle figure at `snort`, then a tiny heroic tag of Anger's theme as he goes serious.
- `endcard` (5 s): Anger's theme, full and heroic, one last time — ending on a dry button.
Write build/music/envelopes.json (rms/low/high/onsets) for every cue.

## 7. Visual style
Same rendering approach as `../if-you-have-time` (clean 2D vector, soft cel shading, smooth_path, thin
contours), but darker, painterly cave palette. Portrait framing: faces in the upper-middle third.
Compression budget ~330 kbps: smooth gradients, sparse particles, no full-frame noise.
