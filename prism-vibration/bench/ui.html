What happens when you shake a rainbow?

Shine white light through a glass prism onto a wall, then vibrate the prism or the light: left and right, up and down, or both. Every frame is an exact 3D ray trace through SF11 flint glass, with colours mixed the way your eye mixes light.

    Model: equilateral 60° prism of Schott SF11 glass (Sellmeier dispersion, n = 1.85 at 400 nm, 1.77 at 700 nm), exact 3D vector Snell's law, 6500 K white light, CIE 1931 colour matching, additive mixing. “Your eye” averages over whole vibration cycles, which is what you see when the motion is faster than about 50 per second. The light source pivots from 20 cm in front of the prism. The numbers in the colour table are measured from the simulation.
